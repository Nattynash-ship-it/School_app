How many calories a day should Natasha plan per adult for the pantry, and what range does the Dietary Guidelines give for adult women?	Plan on 2,000 a day; the Dietary Guidelines for Americans give adult women about 1,600 to 2,400 calories a day
How many days of supplies does NYC Emergency Management tell households to keep?	At least 3 days to shelter in place; 3 to 7 days recommended. This course targets 14 days, then 90
What is the first item to buy for the emergency pantry?	A manual can opener, tested on a can the day you buy it (Ready.gov kit list)
Name five no-cook pantry foods from the Ready.gov list.	Peanut butter, protein or fruit bars, dry cereal or granola, dried fruit, canned juices, shelf-stable pasteurized milk, ready-to-eat canned meats, fruit and vegetables
Why are rice, pasta and dry beans stage-2 food in an apartment?	They need water and fuel; in a blackout the stove may be off and every gallon is carried up the stairs
What does FIFO mean for the pantry?	First in, first out: mark the purchase month on the lid, new cans at the back, take from the front
How long do high-acid canned foods (tomatoes, fruit, juice) keep at best quality?	12 to 18 months (USDA)
How long do low-acid canned foods (meat, fish, beans, vegetables) keep?	2 to 5 years (USDA)
Which cans go in the trash unopened?	Bulging, leaking, spurting, badly dented at the seam or heavily rusted. Never taste to check
What storage temperature harms canned food?	Above 100 F sharply raises spoilage risk; keep cans cool and dry, away from radiators and the stove
What is the safest infant formula for an emergency and why?	Ready-to-feed: it is sterile and needs no water. Powdered formula is not sterile and needs safe water
How do you make water safe for powdered formula if there is no bottled water?	Boil for 1 minute and let it cool before mixing (CDC)
What blood sugar reading counts as low, and what is the 15-15 rule?	Below 70 mg/dL is low. Give 15 g fast carbohydrate, wait 15 minutes, recheck, repeat; then a balanced snack
Name three pantry items that give 15 grams of fast carbohydrate.	4 oz (half cup) juice or regular soda; 1 tablespoon sugar, honey or syrup; 3 to 4 glucose tablets
When is low blood sugar severe and what does it need?	Below 55 mg/dL: injectable glucagon if available and a 911 call. Nothing by mouth if confused or unable to swallow
How long does an unopened refrigerator keep food safe in an outage?	About 4 hours (FDA). Use fridge food first, then freezer, then pantry
How long does a freezer keep food safe with the door shut?	About 48 hours if full, about 24 hours if half full (FDA)
What is the 2-hour rule for perishable food?	Discard perishables held above 40 F for 2 hours or more; 1 hour if the temperature is above 90 F
What temperatures should the fridge and freezer hold, and how do you know?	Fridge at or below 40 F, freezer at or below 0 F; an appliance thermometer in each
When is thawed food safe to refreeze after an outage?	If it still has ice crystals or reads 40 F or below; quality may drop but it is safe (USDA)
How much dry ice holds a freezer, and what are the handling rules?	50 lb holds an 18-cubic-foot freezer about 2 days; never touch with bare hands, never in direct contact with food
Where may a generator, charcoal grill or camp stove be used?	Never indoors, in a basement or garage; outdoors only, more than 20 feet from any window, door or vent (CDC)
What are the CDC symptoms of carbon monoxide poisoning?	Headache, dizziness, weakness, upset stomach, vomiting, chest pain, confusion. Get outside and call 911
What does NYC Local Law 7 of 2004 require of your landlord?	At least one working CO alarm within 15 feet of the primary entrance to each sleeping room
Where is charcoal grilling legal at an NYC apartment building?	Terrace or backyard only, 10 feet from the building, with an extinguisher or hose; never on a balcony, roof or fire escape
May a standard propane grill be kept on an apartment balcony or roof?	No; it is illegal even to store one there. Only a propane container under 16.4 oz may be used briefly on a roof
What should you write on the fridge the moment the power fails?	The time. The FDA safety windows count from when the power stopped, not from when you noticed
What does NYC Parks rule 1-04 say about picking plants?	No severing, mutilating, killing or removing any plant or vegetation on Parks land without the Commissioner's permission; foraging in parks is prohibited
What did the state soil study of 54 NYC community gardens find?	In 70% of gardens at least one sample exceeded health guidance for lead or other metals; raised beds with clean soil and fruiting crops were lower
How do you tell poison hemlock from wild carrot?	Hemlock: smooth hairless stem with purple blotches, no carrot smell, 4 to 10 ft. Wild carrot: hairy stem, carrot smell, 1 to 4 ft
How do you recognise pokeweed, and how toxic is it?	Dark purple berry clusters on bright red non-woody stems; all parts poisonous, about 10 berries can be toxic to an adult
What is the rule on wild mushrooms in this course, and why?	Never eat any. The death cap causes about 90% of fatal mushroom poisonings, symptoms are delayed, and it has poisoned people in New York
What are the NYC Poison Control Center numbers?	212-POISONS (212-764-7667) or the national Poison Help line 1-800-222-1222, free, 24 hours; 911 if unconscious or not breathing normally
Which two lawn weeds does the course name as having no poisonous lookalike, and what are they good for?	Dandelion and broadleaf plantain; vitamins only, never calories, never from a park or roadside, always washed and cooked
How do you identify a dandelion?	Flat rosette of deeply toothed leaves; each yellow flower on its own single hollow leafless stalk with milky sap
Who needs a New York freshwater fishing licence and what does it cost?	Everyone 16 and older; 2026 resident annual $25 (16 to 69), $5 at 70 and over, via DECALS
What is needed to fish the harbour, East River or Hudson?	Enrolment in the free annual Recreational Marine Fishing Registry (NYS DEC)
Who counts as the sensitive population in NYS fish advisories?	People who may become pregnant (under age 50) and children under 15
Which NYC fish are on the avoid-or-eat-less list, and which is banned outright?	Avoid or eat less: American eel, bluefish, carp, striped bass, weakfish, white and channel catfish, white perch. No eels for food from the East, Harlem or tidal Hudson
What did the April 2026 Hudson River advisory update change?	Falling PCB levels let the sensitive population eat up to one 8-oz meal a month of striped bass and some other species from the lower Hudson; check the current table
How risky are raw sprouts and what does the FDA advise?	53 outbreaks 2000-2020, 1,498 ill, 5 dead; cook sprouts thoroughly, high-risk people avoid raw sprouts, use seeds sold for sprouting
How long does white rice store?	Up to 10 years at a steady 70 F with oxygen absorbers; up to 30 years sealed oxygen-free in cool storage (USU Extension)
How long do dry beans store, and what is the catch?	20 to 30 years sealed with oxygen absorbers and kept cool; older beans take longer to cook and may never soften
Why must red kidney beans be boiled, and for how long?	Raw beans contain phytohaemagglutinin; 4 or 5 undercooked beans can cause severe vomiting; boil at 212 F for at least 10 minutes. Slow cookers (about 167 F) are not enough
What is the rationing rule about water and food?	Never ration water to stretch food: a gallon per person per day comes first, then divide calories across the honest number of days
