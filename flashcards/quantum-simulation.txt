What is the Hamiltonian simulation problem?	Given H = ∑ H_j, time t and error ε, build a circuit V with ‖V − e^(−iHt)‖ ≤ ε using poly(n, t, 1/ε) gates.
Why is simulating a general n-qubit system hard classically?	A general state needs 2^n complex amplitudes; 50 qubits need about 18 PB at 16 bytes per amplitude.
Who proved that quantum computers can efficiently simulate local Hamiltonians, and when?	Seth Lloyd, Universal Quantum Simulators, Science 273 (1996), confirming Feynman's 1982 conjecture.
What is the first-order Lie-Trotter formula?	e^(−i(A+B)t) ≈ (e^(−iAt/n) e^(−iBt/n))^n.
First-order Trotter error bound?	‖error‖ ≤ (t²/2n)‖[A,B]‖; it falls as 1/n and grows as t².
What is the second-order (Strang/Suzuki) step?	S2(δ) = e^(−iAδ/2) e^(−iBδ) e^(−iAδ/2); total error O(t³/n²).
When is a Trotter product exact?	When the terms commute ([A,B] = 0); the sizes of the terms do not matter.
Which quantity controls Trotter error: term size or commutator?	The commutator [A,B] (nested commutators for higher orders).
How do you compile e^(−iθ Z⊗Z)?	CNOT(0→1), RZ(2θ) on qubit 1, CNOT(0→1).
What gate implements e^(−iθX)?	RX(2θ), because RX(φ) = e^(−iφX/2).
Two-qubit TFIM, t = 1: steps for error ≤ 0.01?	First order n = 71 (142 CNOTs); second order n = 8 (18 CNOTs after merging half-steps). Computed with scipy.
What does a log-log slope of −2 in error vs n tell you?	The product formula is second order (error ∝ 1/n²).
Name two post-Trotter simulation methods.	Linear combinations of unitaries (LCU) and qubitization with quantum signal processing; also randomized qDRIFT.
What is a spin-orbital?	A spatial orbital combined with a spin state (up or down); each spatial orbital gives two.
How many qubits for H2 in STO-3G under Jordan-Wigner?	4: two spatial orbitals (σg, σu) × 2 spins.
Canonical anticommutation relations?	{a_p, a_q†} = δ_pq and {a_p, a_q} = 0.
Jordan-Wigner form of a_j?	a_j = Z_0 Z_1 ⋯ Z_(j−1) (X_j + iY_j)/2, with |1⟩ = occupied.
Why does Jordan-Wigner need the Z string?	It supplies the fermionic sign so operators on different modes anticommute; without it they commute.
JW number operator a_j† a_j?	(I − Z_j)/2 = |1⟩⟨1|_j.
JW form of neighbour hopping a_0†a_1 + a_1†a_0?	(X_0X_1 + Y_0Y_1)/2.
How many Pauli strings in the JW H2/STO-3G Hamiltonian at 0.735 Å?	15 (computed with PySCF + numpy): identity, 4 single Z, 6 ZZ, 4 XXYY-type.
H2/STO-3G at 0.735 Å: HF and exact energies?	E_HF = −1.1170 Ha, E_FCI = −1.1373 Ha; correlation −20.3 mHa ≈ −12.7 kcal/mol.
What is the H2/STO-3G ground state in JW (qubits σg↑ σg↓ σu↑ σu↓)?	0.9938|1100⟩ − 0.1115|0011⟩.
What is chemical accuracy?	1 kcal/mol ≈ 1.6 mHa error in energy differences, within the chosen model.
Hartree to kcal/mol conversion?	1 Ha = 627.5 kcal/mol.
Which encoding shortens JW's Z strings to about log N?	The Bravyi-Kitaev encoding.
Best-known published FeMoco resource estimate (Lee et al., PRX Quantum 2021)?	About four million physical qubits and under 4 days, assuming 1 μs cycles and 0.1% error rates.
What does HHL output?	A quantum state |x⟩ ∝ A⁻¹|b⟩, not the classical vector x.
HHL key equation for |x⟩?	|x⟩ ∝ ∑ (β_j/λ_j)|u_j⟩ with β_j = ⟨u_j|b⟩.
HHL ancilla success probability?	P = ∑ |β_j|² C²/λ_j², as low as about 1/κ²; amplitude amplification needs about κ rounds.
What is the condition number κ?	|λ|max / |λ|min; it measures how close A is to singular.
Original HHL cost (as usually quoted)?	About log(N) s² κ²/ε, versus classical conjugate gradient about N s √κ log(1/ε) for positive-definite A.
The four caveats of HHL (Aaronson, 2015)?	Input loading of b, output readout of x, condition number κ, and sparsity/efficient simulation of A.
What is dequantization?	A classical algorithm with the same sampling access (ℓ²-norm sampling) matching a quantum algorithm up to polynomial slowdown, e.g. Tang 2018.
Does dequantization remove the speed-up of sparse, full-rank HHL?	No: that problem is BQP-complete; dequantization targets low-rank problems with sampling access.
