How much water should you store per person per day, and for how long?	At least 1 gallon per person per day; 3 days is the minimum, 14 days (14 gallons per person) is the goal (Ready.gov, CDC)
How does the daily water need change in very hot weather?	Ready.gov: needs can double in very hot temperatures; plan about 2 gallons a day in a summer blackout
Who needs more than the base gallon per day?	Children, nursing mothers and sick people (Ready.gov); anyone in a hot apartment
How do you sanitize a food-grade container before filling it with water?	Wash with dish soap, rinse, sanitize with 1 teaspoon unscented bleach in 1 quart of water, rinse well, fill, write the date
How often should water you bottled yourself be replaced?	Every six months (Ready.gov); store-bought bottled water: keep in original container until the printed date
How much does 14 gallons of water weigh?	About 117 lb (8.34 lb per gallon); store it low and on the floor, never on a high shelf
How many cases of 24 half-litre bottles make a 14-day supply for one person?	Five cases (each case is about 3.2 gallons)
Name three places to store water in a small apartment and one place never to store it.	Under the bed, closet floor, behind the sofa; never under the sink beside chemicals, never on a fire escape or by a radiator
Why did high-rise apartments lose water in the 14 August 2003 blackout?	The electric pumps that fill rooftop tanks stopped; gravity emptied the tanks floor by floor and nothing refilled them
What is the difference between potable and non-potable water?	Potable is safe to drink; non-potable can flush toilets and wash floors but must not go in your mouth
How do you sign up for NYC water-main and boil-water alerts?	Text NOTIFYNYC to 692-692, or text your ZIP code to 692-692; app or a858-nycnotify.nyc.gov
Why should stored water be kept away from gasoline, pesticides and cleaners?	Plastic can absorb fumes; contaminated water cannot be fixed by boiling
In a blackout on a high floor, what is the very first action for water?	Plug the bathtub and run the cold tap; fill pots and bottles; the rooftop tank drains top floors first once the pump stops
Why do New York buildings above about six stories lose water in a blackout?	Street pressure only reaches about the sixth floor; higher floors depend on an electric pump filling a rooftop tank
List the EPA's safe alternative water sources in order.	Bottled water; melted ice cubes; the hot-water tank; water drained from the pipes
How do you drain water from the pipes of an apartment?	Open the highest tap to let air in, then drain from the lowest tap into a clean container
What must you do before draining a water heater?	Turn off its electricity (breaker) or gas, open a hot tap for air, let it cool; do not turn it back on until it is full
Which household water sources does the EPA say never to use?	Toilet flush tanks and bowls, radiators, waterbeds, swimming pools and spas
Can boiling make flood water safe?	No. CDC: flood water may contain sewage and chemicals; EPA: boiling and bleach do not remove chemicals, fuel, salt or heavy metals
Why is the East River not a drinking-water source even after boiling?	It is a salt-water tidal strait with combined sewer overflows; boiling concentrates salt; only distillation removes it
What is the penalty for opening a fire hydrant without a spray cap in NYC?	Illegal: fine up to $1,000, up to 30 days in jail, or both (NYC DEP)
How is a hydrant opened legally in a NYC heat wave?	An adult 18 or over asks the local firehouse for a free spray cap; it releases 20-25 gallons a minute instead of more than 1,000
What are the rules during a boil-water notice?	Rolling boil 1 minute then cool; boiled or bottled water for drinking, teeth, food, formula; discard ice made during it; usually 24-48 hours
Is rainwater safe to drink straight from a bowl?	No. CDC: rainwater is not necessarily safe; filter and disinfect it; catch it in the open, not from a roof or gutter
Where do you report a water-main break or an open hydrant in NYC?	311 (call, app or online); the DEP responds
How long do you boil water to make it safe in New York City?	Rolling boil for 1 minute (3 minutes only above 6,500 ft, which is not NYC); let it cool
What is the bleach dose for one gallon of clear water?	8 drops (a little less than 1/8 teaspoon) of plain unscented 5-9% bleach; stir, wait 30 minutes
What is the bleach dose for one gallon of cloudy water?	Settle and pour through cloth first, then 16 drops (about 1/4 teaspoon); wait 30 minutes
What is the bleach dose for one quart of water?	2 drops if clear, 4 drops if cloudy; wait 30 minutes
Which bleach must never be used to treat drinking water?	Scented, colour-safe, splashless or added-cleaner bleach, and pool chlorine; only plain unscented 5-9% sodium hypochlorite
Treated water has no chlorine smell after 30 minutes. What now?	Repeat the dose and wait 15 more minutes; if still no smell the bleach may be spent, so boil, filter and disinfect, or use sunlight
What contaminants do boiling and bleach NOT remove?	Fuel, oil, solvents, heavy metals, salt and most chemicals (EPA, CDC); do not treat water that smells of chemicals
What pore size does a filter need to remove Cryptosporidium and Giardia?	Absolute 1 micron or smaller (CDC); a nominal 1-micron filter can pass 20-30% of 1-micron particles
What do most portable water filters miss, and what do you do about it?	Viruses (and often bacteria); add a chemical disinfectant such as bleach after filtering, or boil
How does solar disinfection (SODIS) work?	Clear water in clear plastic bottles laid on their side in direct sun for at least 6 hours; 2 consecutive days if cloudy; no effect on chemicals
What is the only home method that removes salt and most chemicals?	Distillation: boil in a pot with the lid upside down so steam drips into a cup; slow, and still misses some volatile chemicals
What is the order of treatment for dirty water?	Filter or settle to make it clear, then disinfect (boil, bleach or sun); never treat chemical-smelling water
Why is the bleach kit, not the stove, the reliable NYC method?	Many apartments have electric stoves, gas can be shut off building-wide, and no flame can be run indoors without carbon monoxide risk
Who do you call if someone swallows strong bleach?	911 if they are struggling; Poison Control 1-800-222-1222
Does a carbon pitcher filter make water safe from germs?	No; it improves taste and removes some chemicals but is not a disinfection device
Can a camping stove be used indoors to boil water in a blackout?	No; carbon monoxide from a flame in a closed apartment kills; use it at a wide-open window or balcony with a battery CO alarm, or use bleach
