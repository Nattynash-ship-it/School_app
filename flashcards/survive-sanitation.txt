How much water does a bucket flush take, and where does it go?	1-2 gallons of non-drinking water poured fast into the bowl (not the tank); older toilets may need 3-5 gallons
When must you stop flushing even if you have water?	When water backs up into a tub, sink or floor drain: the sewer is blocked. Switch to the bucket toilet and call 311
What is a twin-bucket toilet?	Two 5-gallon buckets: one bag-lined with absorbent for solids, one for urine, plus a snap-on seat. Separating them keeps the solids bag dry and light
How much cat litter goes in the solids bag?	About half a cup in the bottom, then half a cup after every use, until nothing is visible
Name four absorbents for a bucket toilet.	Plain clay cat litter, sawdust, peat moss, shredded paper, wood ash, garden lime (lime is caustic: gloves, follow the label)
When should a solids bag be tied off?	At about a third full or at the end of each day; fuller bags are heavy and split
How is a used toilet bag stored in an apartment?	Tied, double-bagged, tied again, in a hard container with a tight lid, away from food, children and pets, not on the fire escape or in the hallway
When can bagged human waste go out with regular trash?	Only after the city announces it may, in a declared emergency; until then hold it and ask 311 or watch Notify NYC
Why never tip a bucket into a toilet that will not flush?	A blocked sewer sends it back up into the lowest apartment in the building, often a basement unit
How does Natasha get city instructions on waste disposal in an emergency?	Text NOTIFYNYC to 692-692 (Notify NYC) or call 311
How long should hands be scrubbed with soap?	At least 20 seconds; hum Happy Birthday twice
What alcohol strength must hand sanitizer have?	At least 60% alcohol, printed on the label; soap and water are still better
What is a tippy-tap?	A bottle or jug with a small hole or a loosened cap that gives a thin stream; a full handwash uses a cup or two of water
What happens to handwashing rinse water?	Catch it in a basin; it becomes the next flush water
What container does NYC Health say garbage belongs in?	Hard plastic, rat-resistant containers with tight-fitting lids, never loose bags
What is the NYC residential trash set-out time since April 2023?	After 8 p.m., as close to pick-up as possible
How do you report rats in your building?	File a rodent complaint through 311 with the address; it goes to the Health Department, and the owner must keep the property pest-free
Why does a high-rise lose water in a blackout?	Water is pumped to upper floors; without power the pumps stop. Fill the tub the moment the lights go out
Bleach dose to disinfect surfaces?	5 tablespoons (1/3 cup) per gallon, or 4 teaspoons per quart, of room-temperature water
How long must a bleached surface stay wet?	At least 1 minute, visibly wet; clean with soap first, then disinfect
How long does diluted bleach stay effective?	Up to 24 hours; make a fresh batch every day
Bleach dose for mold on a hard surface?	No more than 1 cup per gallon of water, or just soap and water; scrub, rinse, dry
Bleach dose to make clear water drinkable?	8 drops (about 1/8 teaspoon) per gallon; 16 drops (1/4 teaspoon) if cloudy; wait 30 minutes
What kind of bleach is safe to use for all three jobs?	Regular unscented household bleach, 5-9% sodium hypochlorite; never scented, colour-safe or splash-less with added cleaners
What must bleach never be mixed with?	Ammonia (in many glass cleaners) or any other cleaner; the gas can cause serious injury or death
What is the CDC mold time rule after a flood?	Dry everything within 24-48 hours; if you could not, assume mold is growing
Which flooded items cannot be saved after 48 hours wet?	Porous ones: carpet and padding, upholstery, mattresses, insulation, drywall, wallpaper, cardboard, books
At what size does mold become a professional job?	More than about 10 square feet (roughly 3 ft by 3 ft): the EPA large-job guide applies
How often must standing water be emptied to stop mosquitoes?	At least once a week: buckets, flowerpots, saucers, bird baths, toys, tarps, gutters; cover stored water
How do you report chronic standing water in NYC?	Through 311, online or by phone; inspectors respond and can issue tickets
How long is food safe in a shut fridge without power?	About 4 hours
How long is food safe in a freezer without power?	About 48 hours if full, 24 hours if half full, door kept shut
When is perishable food above 40 F thrown out?	After 2 hours; after 1 hour if the room is above 90 F. When in doubt, throw it out

How do you handle clothes that touched floodwater or sewage?	Keep them in a tied bag apart from other laundry until washed in hot water with detergent, separately
