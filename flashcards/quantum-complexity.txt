What is BQP?	Decision problems solved by uniform poly-size quantum circuits, correct with probability ≥ 2/3 on every input (wrong answer probability ≤ 1/3).
State the proven chain of inclusions around BQP.	P ⊆ BPP ⊆ BQP ⊆ PP ⊆ PSPACE ⊆ EXP. All proven; strictness of each link is open (only P ≠ EXP is known).
Who proved BQP ⊆ PSPACE, and how?	Bernstein and Vazirani (1997), via a path sum: add up exponentially many path amplitudes one at a time using polynomial memory.
Who proved BQP ⊆ PP?	Adleman, DeMarrais and Huang (1997).
Why is BPP ⊆ BQP true?	A quantum circuit can make fair coins (H then measure) and run any classical gate reversibly with Toffoli gates.
Why would proving BPP ≠ BQP also prove P ≠ PSPACE?	If P = PSPACE the chain P ⊆ BPP ⊆ BQP ⊆ PSPACE collapses, forcing BPP = BQP.
Is factoring believed NP-complete?	No. It is in NP ∩ co-NP; an NP-complete problem there would imply NP = co-NP.
Is NP ⊄ BQP proven?	No, it is believed. Proving it would prove P ≠ NP. Evidence: the Ω(√N) search lower bound and lack of exploitable structure.
Why does the 2/3 in the definition of BQP not matter?	Repeat and take a majority vote: error falls exponentially in the number of runs (Chernoff bound). Any constant gap above 1/2 works.
Memory to store an n-qubit state vector in double precision?	16 × 2ⁿ bytes: 30 qubits = 16 GiB, 53 qubits ≈ 144 PB, 56 qubits = 1 EiB.
In the query model, what is counted?	Only the number of oracle queries to the input bits; all other computation is free.
Polynomial method: degree of acceptance probability after T queries?	At most 2T (amplitudes have degree ≤ T), so Q(f) ≥ deg~(f)/2.
What is the approximate degree deg~(f)?	The least degree of a real polynomial within 1/3 of f on every Boolean input.
Approximate degree of OR on N bits, and its consequence?	Θ(√N) (Nisan–Szegedy), so any bounded-error quantum search needs Ω(√N) queries: Grover is optimal.
Quantum query complexity of PARITY on N bits?	N/2: the polynomial method gives ≥ N/2, and Deutsch's algorithm on pairs achieves it.
What does BBBV (1997) show?	Unstructured search needs Ω(√N) quantum queries; relative to a random oracle, NP ⊄ BQP with probability 1.
Which separation does Simon's problem give?	O(n) quantum queries vs Ω(2^(n/2)) randomized: an oracle A with BQP^A ⊄ BPP^A.
Define the Forrelation value Φ(f,g).	Φ = N^(−3/2) ∑ₓ,ᵧ f(x)(−1)^(x·y) g(y) for f,g: {0,1}ⁿ → ±1, N = 2ⁿ. It is the |0…0⟩ amplitude of H, O_f, H, O_g, H.
Forrelation query separation (Aaronson–Ambainis 2015)?	1 quantum query vs Ω(√N / log N) classical randomized queries: optimal for one quantum query.
What did Raz and Tal prove (STOC 2019)?	An oracle relative to which BQP is not contained in PH, using a Forrelation variant.
What do oracle separations NOT prove?	Unrelativized separations: some oracles make the classes equal (e.g. a PSPACE oracle gives P^A = BQP^A).
Why are exponential query speed-ups only for partial functions?	For total functions D(f) = O(Q(f)⁶) (Beals et al.), so the gap is at most polynomial.
Define the linear cross-entropy benchmark.	F_XEB = 2ⁿ · mean of P_ideal(xᵢ) over samples − 1. Uniform noise gives 0; ideal Porter–Thomas sampling gives ≈ 1.
Standard error of F_XEB from M samples near uniform output?	About 1/√M, so resolving F = 0.002 at 5σ needs about 6.25 million samples.
First estimate of a noisy circuit's fidelity?	The product of all gate and readout fidelities, e.g. 0.995^1000 ≈ 0.0067.
Google Sycamore 2019 claim?	Nature: 53 qubits, 20 cycles, about 200 s for a million samples vs an estimated 10,000 years classically; F_XEB ≈ 2.24 × 10⁻³.
Main classical responses to Sycamore 2019?	IBM: estimated 2.5 days on Summit (not run). Pan, Chen, Zhang: 1M samples in about 15 h on 512 GPUs, fidelity ≈ 0.0037.
What was Jiuzhang (2020)?	USTC Gaussian boson sampler: 50 squeezed states, 100 modes, up to 76 detected photons, about 200 s vs an estimated 2.5 billion years.
What were Zuchongzhi 2.1 and 3.0?	USTC superconducting RCS: 2.1 used 56 qubits, 20 cycles (PRL 2021); 3.0 used 83 of 105 qubits, 32 cycles (PRL 2025).
What did Google claim for Willow in December 2024?	RCS on a 105-qubit chip in under 5 minutes vs an estimated 10²⁵ years on Frontier, plus below-threshold error correction.
Why does noisy RCS not give scalable advantage?	With constant noise per gate, Aharonov et al. (STOC 2023) sample it classically in polynomial time; scaling needs error correction.
Boson-sampling probabilities are given by what?	|Perm(U_S)|² for n × n submatrices of the interferometer unitary; permanents are #P-hard. GBS uses hafnians.
What complexity evidence supports sampling hardness?	Efficient exact classical sampling would collapse the polynomial hierarchy; approximate-sampling hardness needs extra conjectures.
Top question to ask of any advantage claim?	Is the classical time measured or extrapolated, and for the best known algorithm matched to the device's real fidelity?
