What does basis encoding do with the bitstring 101?	Prepares |101⟩: apply X to each qubit whose bit is 1. One qubit per bit.
Angle encoding: what state does R_Y(θ) make from |0⟩?	cos(θ/2)|0⟩ + sin(θ/2)|1⟩, so P(1) = sin²(θ/2). Note the half-angle.
Why scale angle-encoded features to [0, π] rather than [0, 2π]?	With the half-angle, [0, π] sweeps |0⟩ to |1⟩ once; [0, 2π] maps min and max to states differing only by a sign.
Amplitude encoding of x ∈ ℝ^N: formula and qubit count?	|x⟩ = (1/‖x‖) ∑ x_i|i⟩ on n = log₂N qubits, with ‖x‖ = √(∑x_i²).
Amplitude-encode (2, 1, 0, 2): the state?	Norm 3, so (2/3)|00⟩ + (1/3)|01⟩ + 0|10⟩ + (2/3)|11⟩; probabilities 4/9, 1/9, 0, 4/9.
What information does amplitude encoding throw away?	The overall scale ‖x‖; store it separately if the model needs it.
Gate cost of preparing a general N-amplitude state?	Order N gates, e.g. Möttönen et al.: 2^(n+2) − 4n − 4 CNOTs for n qubits (4,052 at n = 10).
What is the data-loading bottleneck?	Exponential QML speed-ups often assume |x⟩ is ready; building it from classical data generally costs O(N) gates, erasing the saving.
What is QRAM, and does it exist at scale?	A memory queryable in superposition (e.g. bucket-brigade, O(log N) switches per query). Proposed, not built at scale.
Angle-encoded customer (age 34, spend £120) on two qubits: the state?	0.8188|00⟩ + 0.4172|01⟩ + 0.3514|10⟩ + 0.1790|11⟩ (big-endian |q0 q1⟩).
In Qiskit output, which bit is qubit 0?	The rightmost bit (little-endian). Qiskit '01' means q0 = 1, q1 = 0.
Why is a pure angle-encoded state easy to simulate classically?	It is a product state; every probability is a product of one-qubit factors.
Definition of the fidelity quantum kernel?	k(x, x') = |⟨φ(x)|φ(x')⟩|², between 0 and 1, with k(x, x) = 1.
How is a kernel entry estimated on hardware (compute-uncompute)?	Run U(x) then U(x')†, measure all qubits; the fraction of all-zero outcomes estimates k.
Closed form of the product angle-encoding kernel?	k(x, x') = ∏_j cos²((x_j − x'_j)/2), O(d) classical work.
Standard error of a kernel entry estimated with N shots?	√(k(1 − k)/N), a binomial proportion.
Kernel entry for customers A (34, £120) and B (52, £300)?	cos²(0.4560)·cos²(0.7069) = 0.8061 × 0.5782 = 0.4661.
Three properties every valid kernel (Gram) matrix has?	Symmetric, ones on the diagonal for a fidelity kernel, positive semidefinite (eigenvalues ≥ 0).
In a quantum-kernel SVM, what runs on the quantum device?	Only estimating kernel entries; the SVM optimisation is classical.
How many distinct kernel entries for m training points?	m(m − 1)/2 off-diagonal entries (symmetry, diagonal = 1).
What is a variational quantum classifier?	Encode x, apply a trainable circuit W(θ), measure ⟨Z⟩ = f(x; θ) ∈ [−1, 1], predict sign f.
State the parameter-shift rule.	∂f/∂θ = [f(θ + π/2) − f(θ − π/2)]/2, exact for gates exp(−iθP/2) with P a Pauli product.
Why is the parameter-shift rule exact?	For such gates f(θ) = a cos θ + b sin θ + c; the shifted difference equals 2(−a sin θ + b cos θ) = 2f'(θ).
Why prefer parameter shift to finite differences on hardware?	Finite differences divide shot noise by 2h (tiny); parameter shift divides by 2. Simulated SD: 0.0099 vs 2.19 at h = 0.01.
Circuits needed per gradient for p parameters with parameter shift?	2p circuits (two shifted runs per parameter), each for N shots.
What is dequantization?	A classical algorithm, given matching input access, matches a quantum algorithm up to polynomial slowdown, removing an exponential speed-up.
What did Ewin Tang dequantize, and with what tool?	Kerenidis-Prakash quantum recommendation systems, using ℓ2-norm sampling access and low rank (STOC 2019).
What is ℓ2-norm sampling of a matrix row?	Pick row i with probability ‖A_i‖²/‖A‖_F², the classical twin of measuring an amplitude-encoded state.
What does the Liu-Arunachalam-Temme result prove?	A rigorous end-to-end quantum kernel speed-up on a constructed dataset built from discrete logarithms.
Where is the clearest proven exponential QML advantage?	Learning from quantum experiments with quantum memory (Huang et al., Science 2022): quantum data, not CSV rows.
What is a barren plateau?	Gradients exponentially small in qubit number for most parameters (McClean et al. 2018), so training needs exponentially many shots.
What is exponential concentration of quantum kernels?	Kernel values for different inputs crowd towards a fixed value exponentially in qubits (Thanasilp et al. 2024).
Mean product angle-encoding kernel for d uniform features on [0, π]?	(1/2 + 2/π²)^d ≈ 0.7026^d: 0.0293 at d = 10, 2.2 × 10⁻⁸ at d = 50.
Key finding of Bowles, Ahmed and Schuld (2024)?	Out-of-the-box classical models generally beat 12 QML models on 160 small datasets; removing entanglement often did as well.
Is there a demonstrated quantum advantage for ML on classical tabular data?	No. Advantages are proven only for constructed problems or quantum data; heuristic models have not beaten tuned classical baselines.
95% CI half-width for accuracy 0.84 on 100 test points?	1.96 × √(0.84 × 0.16/100) ≈ ±0.072.
Four checks when reading a QML benchmark?	Tuned classical baseline, error bars and test size, simulator vs hardware with shots reported, no leakage in preprocessing or tuning.
