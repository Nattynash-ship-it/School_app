How many NYC hurricane evacuation zones are there, and which is highest risk?	Six zones, 1 to 6; zone 1 is highest risk and evacuated first; zone 6 floods only in the most severe, unlikely hurricane
When did NYC switch from evacuation zones A, B, C to numbers?	In 2013
Where do you look up your hurricane evacuation zone online?	finder.nyc.gov/hurricane or nyc.gov/knowyourzone
What do the NYC evacuation zones measure?	Risk of storm-surge (sea water) flooding, not rain flooding
Phone numbers for a zone lookup by 311 if you are Deaf or use TTY?	Video Relay Service 212-639-9675; TTY 212-504-4115
Wind forecast that triggers the MTA hurricane plan to suspend service?	Sustained winds of 39 mph or more; above 60 mph some bridges may close
How long does a full MTA subway shutdown take?	About eight hours
When did the MTA shut down for Hurricane Sandy in 2012?	Started 7 p.m. Sunday; all service stopped by 3 a.m. Monday, before the storm
Hurricane Ida (1-2 Sep 2021): NYC deaths and where most drowned?	14 deaths; 10 drowned in basement apartments
Ida rainfall rate versus what NYC sewers were designed for?	3.5 inches per hour versus 1.75 inches per hour
What happened to high-rise water in the 14 August 2003 blackout?	Pumps stopped, so high-rises lost water pressure
Who should you go to first when your zone is ordered to evacuate?	A friend or relative outside every evacuation zone; public centers are for people with no other option
What is a hurricane evacuation center in NYC's plan?	A school or public building where evacuees are processed, then transported to a hurricane shelter if they need one
Only current list of open evacuation centers?	finder.nyc.gov/hurricane or 311 at the time; centers open per emergency
NYC Open Data dataset ids for hurricane evacuation centers?	p5md-weyf (list) and ayer-cga7 (map) at data.cityofnewyork.us
How do you get accessible transport to an evacuation center?	Call 311 as soon as the evacuation order is issued
Are pets allowed at NYC evacuation shelters?	Yes, service animals and pets are welcome; pets are kept in a separate area, so bring a carrier, food, leash and medication
How much medication should you bring to an evacuation center?	At least a week's supply, plus medical supplies or equipment you use regularly
Staten Island evacuation centers seen in a 2015 NYCEM brochure?	IS 51 (20 Houston St), Curtis HS (105 Hamilton Ave), PS 80 Petrides (715 Ocean Terrace); confirm via 311 or the finder at the time
When do NYC cooling centers open?	When the National Weather Service issues a Heat Advisory or Excessive Heat Warning for NYC, or the city declares a heat emergency
Where is the NYC cooling center finder?	finder.nyc.gov/coolingcenters (also nyc.gov/cooloptions), or call 311
Define Code Blue.	In effect when the temperature including wind chill is at or below 32 F between 4 p.m. and 8 a.m.
What changes during Code Blue?	DHS drop-in centers take as many people as possible, shelter intake is simplified, and more than 50 hospitals keep an open-door policy
Manhattan 24-hour drop-in center at 257 West 30th Street?	Olivieri Center; confirm via 311 at the time
Brooklyn drop-in center in the DHS pocket guide?	The Gathering Place, 2402 Atlantic Ave; confirm via 311 at the time
DHS intake center for families with children?	PATH, 151 East 151st St, Bronx; confirm via 311 at the time
DHS intake for single adult men?	8 East 3rd St, Manhattan; confirm via 311 at the time
How many NYC Health + Hospitals acute-care hospitals have emergency departments?	11: Bellevue, Elmhurst, Harlem, Jacobi, Kings County, Lincoln, Metropolitan, North Central Bronx, Queens, South Brooklyn Health, Woodhull
Signs of heat stroke and the action?	Confusion, hot skin, temperature 103 F or above, may stop sweating: call 911 and cool at once; no fluids if confused
How far from windows and doors must a generator run?	Outdoors only, at least 20 feet from any window, door or vent, never in a garage
How do you sign up for Notify NYC by text?	Text NOTIFYNYC to 692-692, then reply with your ZIP code for neighbourhood alerts
Notify NYC text keywords for Spanish and French?	NOTIFYNYCESP and NOTIFYNYCFRE to 692-692
NOAA Weather Radio station and frequency for New York City?	KWO35 on 162.550 MHz, transmitting from the Empire State Building
911 or 311: which is for a life-threatening emergency?	911 for life-threatening emergencies, crime in progress and fire; 311 for all other city services
Since when has Text-to-911 worked in NYC, and in which languages?	Since June 2020, in English and Spanish, in all five boroughs
What should the first Text-to-911 message contain?	The emergency, full address with cross streets, and borough, in complete words; no photos, video, GIFs, emoji or group chats
Text-to-911 slogan?	Call if you can, text if you can't
Which river crossings never allow pedestrians?	Vehicle tunnels: Lincoln, Holland, Queens-Midtown, Hugh L. Carey
Three parts of a plan that works without a phone?	A near meeting place, a far meeting place outside every zone, and an out-of-city contact everyone texts
What does a flooded NYC street hide?	Open manholes and drains, live wires, sewage, subway stairs and cellar hatches
