What is the first sign that it is time to leave a crowd?	The moment you first feel uncomfortable; once a surge starts nobody can leave
At roughly what crowd density does a crush become deadly?	About six people per square metre
How do people actually die in a crowd crush?	Standing up, pressed so tightly from every side that the chest cannot expand to breathe; not by trampling
What should you do with your arms when a crowd starts to pack in?	Bring them up in front of your chest like a boxer, elbows out, to keep space to breathe
Which direction should you move to escape a surging crowd?	With the wave, then diagonally toward an edge between waves; never against the flow
You drop your phone in a packed crowd. What do you do?	Leave it. Bending down is how people fall and get buried
You fall in a crowd crush and cannot get up. What position?	On your side, knees to chest, arms over head and neck
What is the goal of de-escalation with a threatening stranger?	Distance, not victory; end up further away and unhurt
Someone demands your bag on the street. What do you do?	Hand it over, toss it slightly to one side, step away toward light and people, then call or text 911
When should you text 911 instead of calling in NYC?	When speaking would make you a target, e.g. while hiding: call if you can, text if you can't
Who is legally allowed to carry pepper spray in New York?	Adults 18+ with no felony or assault convictions, bought in person in NY State from a licensed firearms dealer or pharmacist
Is it legal to order pepper spray online from another state into New York?	No; it must be bought in person inside New York State
What is required to carry a concealed handgun in New York?	A New York Pistol License; you must be 21 or older
What does disaster research say about looting after storms and floods?	It is rare, the exception; disasters mostly trigger cooperation and altruism (Disaster Research Center; 2025 BMC Public Health review)
Why is the looting myth dangerous?	Fear of looting is a known reason people refuse to evacuate; during Ida, 10 of 14 NYC deaths were basement drownings
How many people died in the 1995 Chicago heat wave?	739
What did Klinenberg find about who died in the 1995 Chicago heat wave?	Isolated elderly people living alone, often with windows shut from fear of crime; living alone led to dying alone
Where do most heat-stroke deaths in NYC happen?	At home, without air conditioning
How often should you check on an elderly or heat-sensitive neighbour in a heat wave?	At least twice a day
Signs of heat stroke in a neighbour?	Confusion, hot skin, may have stopped sweating, temperature 103 F or above: call 911 and cool immediately
Should you give water to a confused, hot neighbour?	No; a confused or unconscious person can choke. Cool the body and call 911
Signs of heat exhaustion and the response?	Heavy sweating, weakness, nausea, cool clammy skin: move to a cool place, loosen clothing, sip water
Which apartments do you knock on first when the elevator dies?	The 'needs help to leave' rows: oxygen, wheelchair or walker, dialysis, blind or deaf, infants, over 75, lives alone
Name four columns of the building contact sheet	Names and apartment; phone plus out-of-town contact; needs help; can help (skills); has kit; building shutoffs and super; outside numbers
NYC CERT age and residency requirements?	18 or older; live or work in New York City; pass an NYC Emergency Management background check
What must a CERT applicant complete before basic training?	FEMA's free online IS-100.c (Introduction to the Incident Command System) and a virtual orientation
How long is NYC CERT basic training and what does it cover?	11 sessions: basic fire safety, search and rescue, traffic management, first aid and triage
How do you apply to NYC CERT?	nyc.gov/cert or call 311
What is 'Be a Buddy'?	A NYC Health-funded pilot in Hunts Point (The Point CDC) that trains residents to check on a vulnerable neighbour during heat, cold or floods
When is Code Blue in effect in NYC?	When the temperature including wind chill is at or below 32 F between 4 p.m. and 8 a.m.; shelters accept walk-ins
How do you sign up for Notify NYC?	Text NOTIFYNYC to 692-692; text your ZIP code to 692-692 for neighbourhood alerts
How does a Wireless Emergency Alert reach your phone?	Broadcast from nearby cell towers by an authorised government agency through your carrier; free, automatic, no sign-up
How do you recognise a WEA on your phone?	A loud tone and vibration, both repeated twice, plus a short message; it cannot be forwarded or texted to you
Where does NYC's official weather warning come from online?	The National Weather Service New York office at weather.gov/okx
What are the steps of the 60-second check on an alarming message?	Who is the source? Open the official page yourself. Find a second official channel. Call 311. Check the date. Then act or refuse to forward
What does a real NYC evacuation order name?	The zone numbers (1 to 6), delivered through Notify NYC and Wireless Emergency Alerts, confirmable via 311
FTC red flags for a post-disaster contractor?	Cash up front; says no licence needed; sign-now discount; insists on wire, gift card, payment app, crypto or cash; wants final payment before work is done
How should you pay a contractor after a flood?	By credit card or check, to a licensed and insured contractor, with final payment only when the work is done
Does FEMA ever charge for disaster assistance?	Never; FEMA does not solicit or accept money, nor does any other government agency
What will a real FEMA inspector never ask for?	Your nine-digit registration number; they already have it. They carry an official ID badge
FEMA Disaster Fraud Hotline number?	1-866-720-5721
Which independent sites does the FTC suggest for checking a charity?	BBB Wise Giving Alliance (Give.org), Charity Navigator, CharityWatch, GuideStar
What does New York's price-gouging law cover?	Unconscionably excessive prices during an abnormal market disruption: food, water, gasoline, generators, batteries, flashlights, lodging, transport; report to the NY Attorney General
FEMA's three-part advice for stopping rumours?	Find trusted sources, share information from trusted sources, discourage others from sharing unverified information
