Define the density operator of an ensemble {(p_i, |ψ_i⟩)}.	ρ = ∑ p_i |ψ_i⟩⟨ψ_i|, a probability-weighted sum of outer products (projectors), not of kets.
What three properties make a matrix a valid density matrix?	Hermitian (ρ† = ρ), positive semidefinite (all eigenvalues ≥ 0) and trace 1.
How do you compute the probability of outcome |m⟩ from ρ?	p(m) = ⟨m|ρ|m⟩; in general p(m) = Tr(M_m† M_m ρ).
How does a density matrix evolve under a unitary gate U?	ρ → UρU†
What is the purity of a state and what range can it take for dimension d?	γ = Tr(ρ²), with 1/d ≤ γ ≤ 1; 1 means pure, 1/d means maximally mixed.
Write a qubit density matrix in terms of its Bloch vector r.	ρ = (I + r_x X + r_y Y + r_z Z)/2 with |r| ≤ 1; r_k = Tr(ρσ_k).
Where do pure and mixed qubit states sit in the Bloch ball?	Pure states on the surface (|r| = 1), mixed states inside (|r| < 1), I/2 at the centre.
What distinguishes |+⟩ from the 50/50 mixture of |0⟩ and |1⟩?	Coherence: ρ01 = 1/2 versus 0. An X-basis measurement gives + always for |+⟩ but half the time for the mixture.
Name two different ensembles that give the qubit state I/2.	50/50 of |0⟩ and |1⟩; 50/50 of |+⟩ and |−⟩ (also three states 120° apart, 1/3 each).
What does the partial trace Tr_B do to a product operator |a1⟩⟨a2| ⊗ |b1⟩⟨b2|?	It gives |a1⟩⟨a2| · ⟨b2|b1⟩: keep A's part, replace B's part by its trace.
Element formula for the reduced state ρ_A of ρ_AB	(ρ_A)_ij = ∑_k ⟨i k|ρ_AB|j k⟩: keep A indices, set B row = B column, sum.
For a pure state with amplitude matrix C (rows A, columns B), what are ρ_A and ρ_B?	ρ_A = C C† and ρ_B = Cᵀ C*.
What is the reduced state of either qubit of a Bell pair?	I/2, the maximally mixed state (purity 1/2), even though the pair is pure.
State the Schmidt decomposition of a bipartite pure state.	|ψ⟩ = ∑ √λ_k |a_k⟩|b_k⟩ with orthonormal {|a_k⟩}, {|b_k⟩}; √λ_k are the singular values of C.
How does the Schmidt rank tell you whether a pure state is entangled?	Rank 1 means product; rank 2 or more means entangled. For two qubits, det C ≠ 0 means entangled.
Define the entanglement entropy of a pure bipartite state.	E = S(ρ_A) = −∑ λ_k log₂ λ_k over the Schmidt weights; 1 ebit for a Bell pair, 0 for a product.
Why is 'ρ_A is mixed' not a general entanglement test?	It only works for pure global states; ½(|00⟩⟨00| + |11⟩⟨11|) has ρ_A = I/2 but is separable.
In Qiskit, what does partial_trace(state, [k]) do?	Traces OUT qubit k (Qiskit little-endian numbering: qubit 0 is the rightmost bit) and returns the rest.
What is a purification of a mixed state ρ_A = ∑ λ_k |a_k⟩⟨a_k|?	A pure state such as ∑ √λ_k |a_k⟩|k⟩ on A plus an extra system whose partial trace gives back ρ_A.
What does CPTP stand for, and why is each part needed?	Completely positive, trace preserving: output must be a valid state even with an entangled ancilla, and probabilities must still sum to 1.
Write the Kraus (operator-sum) form of a channel.	ℰ(ρ) = ∑ K_k ρ K_k† with ∑ K_k† K_k = I.
Kraus operators of the bit-flip channel	√(1−p) I and √p X; shrinks y and z by (1−2p).
What does the depolarizing channel do to the Bloch vector?	Shrinks it uniformly: r → (1−p) r, i.e. ρ → (1−p)ρ + p I/2.
Kraus operators of amplitude damping with strength γ	K0 = [[1, 0], [0, √(1−γ)]], K1 = [[0, √γ], [0, 0]]; fixed point |0⟩.
What does dephasing do on the Bloch ball?	Leaves z unchanged and shrinks x and y: every state moves toward the z axis.
How is T1 defined?	Energy relaxation time: excited population decays as e^(−t/T1); amplitude damping with γ = 1 − e^(−t/T1).
How is T2 defined, and what bound links it to T1?	Coherence |ρ01| decays as e^(−t/T2); 1/T2 = 1/(2T1) + 1/Tφ so T2 ≤ 2T1.
What is the difference between T2* and T2E?	T2* from a Ramsey experiment includes slow drifts; T2E from a Hahn echo cancels them, so usually T2E ≥ T2*.
Give a map that is positive but not completely positive.	The transpose: it maps half of a Bell pair to a matrix with eigenvalue −0.5.
Define the trace distance between ρ and σ.	D = ½ Tr|ρ − σ| = ½ ∑ |eigenvalues of ρ − σ|; for qubits D = |r − s|/2.
What is the operational meaning of trace distance?	Best one-shot chance of telling ρ from σ (equal priors) is ½(1 + D).
Define fidelity (squared convention) and its pure-state forms.	F = (Tr √(√ρ σ √ρ))²; F = ⟨ψ|σ|ψ⟩ for pure ψ; F = |⟨ψ|φ⟩|² for two pure states.
State the Fuchs–van de Graaf inequalities (squared fidelity).	1 − √F ≤ D ≤ √(1 − F), equality on the right for two pure states.
Convert process fidelity to average gate fidelity in dimension d.	F_avg = (d·F_pro + 1)/(d + 1); error per gate r = 1 − F_avg.
What does randomized benchmarking fit, and what does it report?	Survival A·p^m + B versus sequence length m; average error per Clifford r = (d − 1)(1 − p)/d, insensitive to SPAM.
Why can randomized benchmarking understate coherent errors?	Randomisation averages systematic rotations into stochastic noise; repeated coherent errors can grow about quadratically.
Four questions to ask of a published gate fidelity	Best pair or median/mean? Isolated or simultaneous? Average or process fidelity? What error bars and date?
