What does EFFAK stand for, and who publishes it?	Emergency Financial First Aid Kit; a free checklist and forms from Ready.gov and FEMA (ready.gov/emergency-financial-first-aid-kit, or (800) 480-2520)
Name the four sections of the EFFAK.	Household Identification; Financial and Legal Documentation; Medical Information; Household Contacts
Where do Ready.gov and FEMA say to keep paper copies of critical documents?	In a fireproof, waterproof box or safe at home, in a bank safe-deposit box, or with a trusted friend or relative
How should electronic copies of documents be stored?	Password-protected on a flash drive or external drive kept in the fireproof, waterproof box, or in a secure cloud service
Why keep cash in small bills for an emergency?	ATMs and card machines may not work and businesses may not be able to make change (Ready.gov)
What does the Insurance Information Institute say a home inventory should record for each item?	Type of item, original price (receipt if possible), year purchased, and serial number; keep a copy outside the home
What are two-factor backup codes and how many does Google issue?	Single-use codes generated in advance for when the phone is lost; Google gives ten eight-digit codes
Where does Google advise keeping printed backup codes?	Where you keep your passport or other important documents; never share them; each works once
Which account's backup codes should be protected first, and why?	Email, because almost every other account resets through it
What is the 'one-page summary' in the document folder?	A handwritten page with name, date of birth, address, SSN, insurance policy and claims line, bank, doctor, pharmacy and out-of-town contact
How do you sign up for Notify NYC by text?	Text NOTIFYNYC to 692-692; text your ZIP code to 692-692 for neighbourhood alerts
When to call 911 and when to call 311?	911 for a life-threatening emergency; 311 for city services, housing complaints and finding open centers
Does a standard renters insurance policy cover flooding?	No. It covers water from burst pipes or appliance leaks but excludes rising water from outside; flood needs a separate NFIP policy
What is the NFIP?	The National Flood Insurance Program, run by FEMA; renters can buy contents-only cover through an agent (floodsmart.gov)
What is the maximum NFIP contents coverage for a renter?	$100,000 for belongings; the building is the landlord's responsibility
How long is the NFIP waiting period for a new flood policy?	Usually 30 days from purchase; a policy bought days before a storm pays nothing for that storm
First step once a flooded home is safe, per NYC flood-claims guidance?	Take pictures and detailed notes of damage to the home and belongings before cleaning up; then register the claim with the insurer
Should you repair flood damage before the adjuster visits?	No. Stop further damage but make no permanent repairs before talking to the insurer or the adjuster has seen it
What two steps does NYC Emergency Management ask flood-hit residents to take?	Submit a damage report at reportdamage.nyc.gov AND file a 311 service request
Does a NYC damage report replace an insurance claim?	No. It is used only for assessment and planning and does not guarantee financial help
Four ways to apply for FEMA Individual Assistance?	Online at DisasterAssistance.gov (fastest), phone 1-800-621-3362, the FEMA app, or in person at a Disaster Recovery Center
What information should be ready before applying to FEMA?	Home, mailing and email addresses; phone; Social Security number; bank details; insurance information
Why must you file an insurance claim before FEMA will help?	By law FEMA cannot duplicate benefits; it pays only for uninsured or under-insured losses
If the insurance settlement is delayed, can FEMA help?	FEMA may help if the settlement is delayed more than 30 days
How long do you have to send FEMA your insurance settlement or denial?	60 days from the date on the FEMA letter requesting it
How long do you have to appeal a FEMA decision?	60 days from the date of the decision letter
What is a Disaster Recovery Center (DRC)?	A temporary FEMA office near the disaster where staff help one-on-one with applications and documents
How do you find the nearest Disaster Recovery Center by text?	Text DRC and your ZIP code to 43362 (e.g. DRC 11221); or use the locator at FEMA.gov/DRC
What should you bring to a Disaster Recovery Center?	Nothing is required, but insurance information and the address and ZIP of the damaged home make it faster
Which New York State hotline helps with insurance problems after a disaster?	NYS Department of Financial Services Disaster Hotline, (800) 339-1759, in 200-plus languages
What are the dates of NYC heat season?	1 October to 31 May
NYC daytime heat rule (6 a.m. to 10 p.m.)?	Inside at least 68°F whenever the outside temperature is below 55°F
NYC night-time heat rule (10 p.m. to 6 a.m.)?	Inside at least 62°F regardless of the outside temperature
What hot-water temperature must a NYC landlord provide, and when?	At least 120°F at the tap, 365 days a year
How do you report no heat or hot water in NYC?	Call 311, or use the 311 website or app, with address, apartment number and a phone number; HPD contacts the owner and inspects if not restored
What can a tenant do if the landlord ignores HPD violations?	Bring an HP Action in Housing Court to force repairs; get free legal advice first
Who is responsible for building-related flood damage in a NYC rental?	The landlord, under the Housing Maintenance Code (HPD flooding guidance for renters)
Why is a cheap indoor thermometer part of the document chapter?	A dated log of room temperature and every call to the super is the evidence for a heat complaint
What killed most of the New Yorkers who died in Hurricane Ida (Sept 2021)?	Drowning in basement apartments; 10 of 14 deaths. Never store the only copies of documents below street level
