How many complex amplitudes describe a general state of n qubits?	2^n amplitudes, one per bitstring, with the squared magnitudes summing to 1.
In the textbook (big-endian) convention, which vector index holds |10⟩ for two qubits?	Index 2 (binary 10): |1⟩ ⊗ |0⟩ = (0, 0, 1, 0).
In a Qiskit bitstring such as '011', which character is qubit 0?	The rightmost character. Qiskit is little-endian: basis index = sum of q_k · 2^k.
Write CNOT as a sum of tensor products (control first).	CNOT = |0⟩⟨0| ⊗ I + |1⟩⟨1| ⊗ X; on basis states |a, b⟩ → |a, a ⊕ b⟩.
Why is CZ symmetric in its two qubits?	CZ = diag(1, 1, 1, −1) only adds a sign to |11⟩, a condition that treats both qubits the same.
How do you build SWAP from CNOTs?	Three CNOTs with alternating control: CNOT(0→1) · CNOT(1→0) · CNOT(0→1).
What does the Toffoli gate compute?	|a, b, c⟩ → |a, b, c ⊕ ab⟩: a reversible AND; it is universal for classical reversible logic.
What is phase kickback?	If U|u⟩ = e^{iφ}|u⟩, controlled-U on (|0⟩+|1⟩)|u⟩/√2 gives (|0⟩ + e^{iφ}|1⟩)|u⟩/√2: the phase lands on the control.
What does CNOT do to |+⟩|−⟩?	It gives |−⟩|−⟩: X|−⟩ = −|−⟩, so the −1 kicks back onto the control.
When is a pure two-qubit state separable?	When it can be written |a⟩ ⊗ |b⟩; equivalently the 2×2 amplitude matrix has determinant α00α11 − α01α10 = 0.
Which circuit maps |xy⟩ to the Bell state β_xy?	H on qubit 0, then CNOT with qubit 0 as control and qubit 1 as target.
Write the four Bell states.	Φ± = (|00⟩ ± |11⟩)/√2, Ψ± = (|01⟩ ± |10⟩)/√2.
What is the concurrence of a pure two-qubit state?	C = 2|α00α11 − α01α10|; 0 for product states, 1 for maximally entangled states.
How does the Schmidt decomposition relate to the SVD?	Reshape the amplitudes into a matrix; its singular values are the Schmidt coefficients, and the rank tells product (1) versus entangled.
What is the reduced state of one qubit of any Bell state?	The maximally mixed state I/2: each qubit alone is a fair coin.
Why is Z-basis correlation alone not proof of entanglement?	A classical mixture of |00⟩ and |11⟩ gives the same Z statistics; entanglement shows up as correlation in a second basis too.
Write the GHZ and W states for three qubits.	GHZ = (|000⟩ + |111⟩)/√2; W = (|001⟩ + |010⟩ + |100⟩)/√3.
What happens to GHZ and W when one qubit is lost?	GHZ leaves a classical mixture (no entanglement); W leaves a pair that is still entangled (concurrence 2/3).
What are ⟨Z⊗Z⟩, ⟨X⊗X⟩, ⟨Y⊗Y⟩ for the singlet Ψ−?	All three equal −1: the singlet is anticorrelated in every basis.
State the no-cloning theorem.	No unitary U satisfies U(|ψ⟩|s⟩) = |ψ⟩|ψ⟩ for every state |ψ⟩ with a fixed blank |s⟩.
What is the one-line inner-product proof of no-cloning?	Cloning ψ and φ forces ⟨φ|ψ⟩ = ⟨φ|ψ⟩², so the overlap must be 0 or 1.
Which sets of states CAN be copied perfectly?	Mutually orthogonal sets (e.g. |0⟩, |1⟩ with a CNOT), and any state you know classically.
What does CNOT do to |+⟩|0⟩, and why is it not a copy?	It gives the Bell state (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2, not |+⟩|+⟩; fidelity with |++⟩ is 0.5.
What is the best universal fidelity for 1 → 2 qubit cloning?	5/6 per copy (Bužek-Hillery universal cloner, 1996).
What is the no-deleting theorem?	Given two copies |ψ⟩|ψ⟩ of an unknown state, no unitary process yields |ψ⟩|0⟩ (Pati-Braunstein 2000).
Why can entanglement not send a message?	Bob's reduced state Tr_A(|ψ⟩⟨ψ|) is unchanged by anything Alice does locally, so his statistics cannot change.
Give the Helstrom bound for two pure states with equal priors.	P_max = ½(1 + √(1 − |⟨ψ|φ⟩|²)).
Best success probability for distinguishing |0⟩ from |+⟩?	½(1 + 1/√2) ≈ 0.8536, versus 0.75 for a plain Z measurement.
What does it mean for a finite gate set to be universal?	Any unitary can be approximated to any accuracy ε (operator norm) by a circuit from the set.
Which gates generate the Clifford group?	H, S and CNOT; Clifford gates map Pauli products to Pauli products under conjugation.
How many single-qubit Clifford gates are there (up to global phase)?	24, the rotational symmetries of the octahedron with vertices ±X, ±Y, ±Z.
What is T X T†?	(X + Y)/√2, which is not a Pauli, so T is not a Clifford gate.
State the Gottesman-Knill theorem.	Circuits of Clifford gates, Pauli measurements and |0⟩ preparations can be simulated efficiently on a classical computer.
What does Gottesman-Knill teach about entanglement?	Entanglement is necessary but not sufficient for speed-up: Clifford circuits are highly entangled yet classically easy.
What is the Solovay-Kitaev gate-count scaling?	O(log^c(1/ε)) gates to reach accuracy ε; Dawson-Nielsen give c ≈ 3.97.
What is the counting lower bound on approximating single-qubit gates?	Ω(log(1/ε)): g^L sequences must cover about (1/ε)^3 ε-balls in SU(2).
What is the T-count of the standard Toffoli decomposition?	7 (4 T and 3 T†), with 6 CNOTs and 2 H gates.
How do gate errors accumulate in a circuit?	At most linearly: the total operator-norm error is at most the sum of the per-gate errors.
