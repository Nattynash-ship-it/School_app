In Manhattan, which way do the numbered streets run and which way do the numbers rise?	Streets run east-west; numbers rise as you go north (uptown), from 1st Street to 228th Street.
Which avenue divides Manhattan addresses into East and West?	Fifth Avenue (Broadway below 8th Street). Building numbers start again at Fifth Avenue.
How many Manhattan street blocks make about one mile?	About 20 north-south street blocks (each about 264 feet).
How many Manhattan avenue blocks make about one mile?	Roughly 7 avenue blocks; ten avenue blocks is about 1.4 miles.
How far off true north is the Manhattan grid?	About 29 degrees east of true north; 'uptown' is north-north-east.
What does the Queens address 38-01 35th Avenue mean?	On 35th Avenue near 38th Street (number before the hyphen); building 01 on that block (number after the hyphen).
Which river is on the west side of Manhattan and which is on the east?	Hudson River on the west; East River on the east.
How long is the Brooklyn Bridge walk and how long does it take at a regular pace?	About 1.1 miles; about 20-30 minutes without stopping. The walkway is above the traffic lanes.
Which Manhattan neighbourhoods do the Manhattan and Williamsburg Bridges leave from?	Manhattan Bridge from Chinatown to Downtown Brooklyn; Williamsburg Bridge from the Lower East Side to Williamsburg.
Since 18 May 2025, where do pedestrians walk on the Ed Koch Queensboro Bridge?	The south outer roadway is pedestrians only; the north outer roadway is cyclists only.
Name four Harlem River bridges that pedestrians can use to reach the Bronx.	Willis Avenue, Third Avenue, Macombs Dam (West 155th Street) and Broadway Bridge.
At what sustained wind speed may the MTA close its bridges to all traffic?	60 mph or more (certain vehicles are barred from 50 mph).
Where do you check whether NYC Ferry is running?	ferry.nyc/live-ferry-status or the NYC Ferry app; expect boats to stop in high wind.
How should you read a compass in a city of steel?	In an open space away from cars, fences and grates; flat at waist height; take three readings and trust them only if they agree.
What should you do this week to learn your own walking pace?	Time yourself walking 20 Manhattan street blocks with your usual bag; that is your personal mile.
How much moving water can knock an adult off her feet, and how much can carry away a small car?	6 inches of fast-moving water; 12 inches for a small car; 2 feet carries away most vehicles.
What share of flood deaths does the NWS say are vehicle-related?	More than 50 percent.
What is the NWS slogan for flooded roads?	Turn Around, Don't Drown.
How far should you stay from a downed wire, and whom do you call?	Stay 30 feet away, keep others away, call 911 first, then Con Edison at 1-800-752-6633 (1-800-75-CONED).
Why is a flooded NYC street more dangerous than it looks?	It can hide manhole covers pushed off by water pressure and downed wires covered by water or leaves.
When did the MTA stop subways and buses before Hurricane Sandy?	Subways at 7 p.m. and buses at 9 p.m., hours before the storm; people were told to stay off the roads.
How many people died in NYC in Hurricane Ida and where did most drown?	14 deaths; 10 drowned in basement apartments (1-2 September 2021).
What is the ten-mile rule for an evacuation bag?	Carry only what you can walk ten miles with; weigh it and walk 20 blocks with it to test.
Which socks prevent blisters and which cause them?	Moisture-wicking wool or synthetic socks prevent them; cotton holds sweat and rubs.
What do you do the moment you feel a hot spot on your foot?	Stop and cover it with moleskin or tape; change damp socks.
Signs of heat stroke and the first action?	Confusion, hot skin, temperature 103 F or above, may stop sweating; call 911 and cool the person; no fluids if confused.
When is Code Blue in effect in NYC?	When the temperature including wind chill is at or below 32 F between 4 p.m. and 8 a.m.; shelters and drop-in centers accept walk-ins.
What does Ready.gov say to leave when you evacuate?	A note telling others when you left and where you are going; lock doors and windows; unplug small appliances; take your pets.
Should the fridge be unplugged when you evacuate?	No, leave the refrigerator and freezer plugged in unless there is a risk of flooding.
How do you sign up for Notify NYC by text?	Text NOTIFYNYC to 692-692, or text your ZIP code to 692-692 for neighbourhood alerts.
What is the international distress signal?	Three of anything, evenly spaced: three whistle blasts, three flashes, three fires.
What is the SOS pattern in Morse code?	Three short, three long, three short (short about 1 second, long about 3 seconds).
What do the ground-to-air signals X, SOS and an arrow mean?	X = medical emergency; SOS = need help; arrow = direction of travel. Make them large and high-contrast.
Trapped under debris: what does FEMA say to do?	Send a text, bang on a pipe or wall, use a whistle instead of shouting, cover your mouth with your shirt, do not move about or kick up dust.
Why should you not shout when trapped under rubble?	Shouting makes you inhale dangerous amounts of dust and wastes strength; tap and whistle instead.
In what order do you give your location to 911 in NYC?	Borough, street address, cross streets, floor, apartment number, side of the building; then the problem and how many people.
Since when has Text-to-911 worked in NYC?	Since June 2020. Call if you can, text if you can't.
Why use an out-of-town contact in a citywide emergency?	Local lines jam; one long-distance text can get through and the contact relays it to everyone else.
What is the cheapest signalling item to add to your keys?	A plastic pea-less whistle; it works wet and frozen and carries farther than a voice.
