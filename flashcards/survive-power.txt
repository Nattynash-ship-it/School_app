What is the first thing to reach for when the lights go out?	A flashlight or headlamp kept in a fixed place; never a candle (Ready.gov: only flashlights for emergency lighting)
Why switch off the stove, iron and space heater the moment power fails?	They restart by themselves when power returns, possibly while you sleep or are out
Why unplug electronics during an outage, and what do you leave on?	Power can return with surges that damage equipment; leave one lamp or radio on to show when service is back
FDNY: how far must a candle be from anything that burns?	At least 4 feet from curtains, bedding, papers, books and decorations
FDNY: when do you put a candle out?	Before leaving the room or lying down, and when the flame is within 2 inches of the holder
What share of candle fires start with an unattended candle (FDNY)?	More than 33 percent
Why pick one battery size for every light and radio in the kit?	Any spare then fits any device, and you stock only one kind of battery
When should you test the kit's lights and radio and swap weak batteries?	Twice a year, when the clocks change
How often should the power bank be topped up?	Charge it fully now and top it up on the first of every month
Where must a generator run (CDC/CPSC)?	Outdoors only, more than 20 feet from any window, door or vent, exhaust pointed away
Where may a generator never run, even with fans or open windows?	Inside a home, garage, basement, crawlspace or shed
About how many people die each year in the US from portable-generator carbon monoxide (CPSC)?	About 100
What fraction of non-fatal CO poisonings in hurricanes involved generators within 7 feet of the home (CDC)?	Nearly half
How long can carbon monoxide linger indoors after a generator is switched off?	Hours
What kind of CO alarm and where (CPSC)?	Battery-powered or battery-backup, on every level of the home and outside sleeping areas
CO alarm sounds or everyone feels dizzy and sick near a running engine: what do you do?	Get everyone outside into fresh air first, then call 911; do not go back in
FDNY lithium-ion rules for charging a power bank, phone or e-bike?	UL-marked device, manufacturer's cord, no overnight or unattended charging, room temperature, away from flammables, never blocking the exit
How many lithium-ion battery fires and deaths did NYC report in 2023?	268 fires and 18 deaths; 133 fires started in batteries that were not charging
What does NYC Emergency Management say about candles and gas lanterns in an outage?	Use flashlights instead of candles or gas lanterns
Why send a text instead of a call in a disaster (FEMA)?	Phone networks are overloaded; texts need less bandwidth and are more likely to get through
How do you reach 911 in NYC when calls fail?	Text-to-911, available since June 2020: call if you can, text if you can't
What is an out-of-area contact?	One relative or friend outside the region (FEMA: out of state) whom everyone texts with their location and status
How do you sign up for Notify NYC by text?	Text NOTIFYNYC to 692-692, or your ZIP code to 692-692 for neighbourhood alerts
What are Wireless Emergency Alerts (WEA)?	Free, area-targeted government alerts pushed by cell towers; no app or sign-up; not stuck in congestion; keep them switched on
How do you register for and use Con Edison outage texts?	Text REG to OUTAGE (688243) to register; text OUT to 688243 to report an outage
What are the NOAA Weather Radio frequencies?	Seven frequencies: 162.400, 162.425, 162.450, 162.475, 162.500, 162.525 and 162.550 MHz
What does SAME on a weather radio do?	Specific Area Message Encoding: the alarm sounds only for the county you program
Can an ordinary AM/FM radio receive NOAA Weather Radio?	No; you need a receiver built for it (NOAA runs more than 1,000 transmitters)
FRS radio: licence and power limit?	No licence; 2 watts or less (on channels 1-7 and 15-22)
GMRS radio: licence terms?	FCC licence required: $35, valid 10 years, no test; immediate family may use it
Real range of FRS walkie-talkies among city buildings?	About half a mile to a mile (roughly 0.5-1.5 km once buildings block the path), despite 'up to 35 miles' on the box
Con Edison outage phone number?	1-800-75-CONED (1-800-752-6633); also coned.com/reportoutage, the app, or outagemap.coned.com for status
When should you ride an elevator after the lights flicker or power is restored?	Not until the building confirms the elevators have been reset; take the stairs with a headlamp
How many elevators got stuck in the July 2019 Manhattan blackout (FDNY)?	More than 400; everyone was removed safely
Why do upper floors of tall buildings lose water in a blackout?	Electric pumps lift water to the roof tank or upper floors; when they stop, the tank drains and taps run dry
What do you do with water in the first minute of an outage in a high-rise?	Fill the bathtub, pots and bottles while water still flows; use tub water to flush with a bucket
How long does a closed refrigerator keep food safe in an outage (FDA)?	About 4 hours
How long does a freezer keep food frozen with the door shut (FDA)?	About 48 hours if full, about 24 hours if half full
When must perishable food be discarded (FDA)?	Above 40 F for more than 2 hours, or 1 hour if the room is above 90 F; never taste to check
How do you register life-support equipment with Con Edison?	coned.com/SpecialServices or 1-800-75-CONED; clinician certifies within 30 days, renew yearly; register even if electricity is in your rent
What does Con Edison's Life-Sustaining Equipment registration give you, and what does it not?	Contact in emergencies, priority restoration, planned-outage alerts; it does not power the device, so you still need batteries or tanks
When did the 2003 blackout start and how many people lost power?	Just after 4:10 p.m. on 14 August 2003; about 50 million in the Northeast and Ontario
How many people were in subway cars when the MTA stopped in 2003, and when did limited service return?	Up to 400,000; limited subway service around 8 p.m.; full NYC power back on 16 August
If only your apartment is dark and the hallway has power, what is the first check?	Your own circuit-breaker panel, then the super; it is not a Con Edison outage
