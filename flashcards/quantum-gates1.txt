What makes a 2×2 matrix a valid single-qubit gate?	It is unitary: U†U = I, so it preserves total probability and can be undone by U†.
A circuit applies A, then B, then C. What single matrix is it?	C·B·A: the first gate applied sits on the right of the product.
Matrix and action of the Pauli X gate	X = [0 1; 1 0]. X|0⟩ = |1⟩, X|1⟩ = |0⟩ (bit flip, half-turn about the x-axis).
Matrix and action of the Pauli Y gate	Y = [0 −i; i 0]. Y|0⟩ = i|1⟩, Y|1⟩ = −i|0⟩ (half-turn about the y-axis).
Matrix and action of the Pauli Z gate	Z = [1 0; 0 −1]. Z|0⟩ = |0⟩, Z|1⟩ = −|1⟩ (phase flip, half-turn about the z-axis).
Products of two different Pauli matrices	XY = iZ, YZ = iX, ZX = iY; reversing the order flips the sign (they anticommute).
What does the Hadamard gate do to |0⟩ and |1⟩?	H|0⟩ = |+⟩ = (|0⟩ + |1⟩)/√2 and H|1⟩ = |−⟩ = (|0⟩ − |1⟩)/√2.
Conjugation identities for the Hadamard	HXH = Z, HZH = X, HYH = −Y. H swaps the x- and z-axes of the Bloch sphere.
Write H in terms of Pauli matrices	H = (X + Z)/√2.
Define the S and T gates	S = diag(1, i) = P(π/2); T = diag(1, e^(iπ/4)) = P(π/4).
The phase-gate ladder linking T, S, Z and I	T² = S, S² = Z, T⁴ = Z, T⁸ = I.
Global phase versus relative phase	Global phase e^(iθ)|ψ⟩ is unobservable; the phase of |1⟩ relative to |0⟩ is physical (e.g. |+⟩ vs |−⟩).
What is S|+⟩?	|+i⟩ = (|0⟩ + i|1⟩)/√2: S turns the Bloch vector a quarter turn from +x to +y.
Why is T needed for universality?	H, S, CNOT are Clifford gates and are classically simulable (Gottesman–Knill); adding the non-Clifford T gives a universal set.
What is T X T†?	(X + Y)/√2, not a Pauli matrix, which shows T is outside the Clifford group.
Probability of 0 after H, T, H on |0⟩	cos²(π/8) = (1 + cos(π/4))/2 ≈ 0.8536.
Definition of Rx(θ), Ry(θ), Rz(θ)	R_P(θ) = e^(−iθP/2) = cos(θ/2)I − i sin(θ/2)P for P = X, Y, Z.
Matrix of Rz(θ)	diag(e^(−iθ/2), e^(iθ/2)).
Matrix of Ry(θ)	[cos(θ/2) −sin(θ/2); sin(θ/2) cos(θ/2)], the only rotation with all-real entries.
What is Rz(2π) as a matrix?	−I. A full turn gives a global phase of −1; you need 4π for the identity.
How are Rz(φ) and the phase gate P(φ) related?	Rz(φ) = e^(−iφ/2)·P(φ): equal up to global phase, but their controlled versions differ.
State the ZYZ Euler decomposition	Any single-qubit U = e^(iα)·Rz(β)·Ry(γ)·Rz(δ) for real α, β, γ, δ; Rz(δ) acts first.
ZYZ recipe: how do you get α and γ from U?	α = arg(det U)/2; with V = e^(−iα)U, γ = 2·atan2(|V10|, |V00|).
ZYZ recipe: how do you get β and δ?	arg V11 = (β + δ)/2 and arg V10 = (β − δ)/2; solve the two equations.
ZYZ angles of the Hadamard gate	H = e^(iπ/2)·Rz(0)·Ry(π/2)·Rz(π).
Define the U3(θ, φ, λ) gate	[cos(θ/2), −e^(iλ)sin(θ/2); e^(iφ)sin(θ/2), e^(i(φ+λ))cos(θ/2)] = e^(i(φ+λ)/2)Rz(φ)Ry(θ)Rz(λ).
Which U3 angles give the Hadamard?	U3(π/2, 0, π) = H exactly; in Qiskit qc.u(pi/2, 0, pi, 0).
What is H·Rz(θ)·H?	Rx(θ): conjugating by H turns a z-rotation into an x-rotation.
How do you measure a qubit in the X basis?	Apply H, then measure in the computational basis; outcome 0 means |+⟩ (eigenvalue +1).
How do you measure a qubit in the Y basis?	Apply S† then H (matrix H·S†), then measure; outcome 0 means |+i⟩.
Expectation value of a Pauli from shot counts	⟨P⟩ ≈ (n0 − n1)/N, with standard error √((1 − ⟨P⟩²)/N).
How many shots give a standard error of 0.01 when ⟨P⟩ ≈ 0?	About 10,000, since the error is 1/√N at ⟨P⟩ = 0.
One-qubit density matrix from a Bloch vector r	ρ = (I + r_x X + r_y Y + r_z Z)/2 with r = (⟨X⟩, ⟨Y⟩, ⟨Z⟩) and |r| ≤ 1.
Why can one measurement basis not identify a qubit state?	Different states share the same statistics in one basis, e.g. |+⟩ and |+i⟩ both give 50/50 in Z.
Your tomography gives |r| > 1. What went wrong?	Sampling noise; the raw estimate is unphysical (negative eigenvalue). Rescale into the Bloch ball or use maximum likelihood.
How does Qiskit order bits in a counts key?	Little-endian: qubit 0 is the rightmost bit, so X on qubit 0 of 2 qubits gives '01'.
