What is the first question to ask in any apartment emergency before deciding to stay or go?	Is the danger outside the building (stay inside) or inside or beneath it (get out or get up)?
In Hurricane Ida (Sept 2021), how many NYC deaths were there and how many drowned in basement apartments?	14 deaths in NYC; 10 drowned in basement apartments
How fast did rain fall during Ida compared with what NYC sewers were designed for?	3.5 inches per hour, about double the 1.75 inches per hour design capacity
If you live in a basement and a flash flood warning is issued, when do you go upstairs?	Immediately on the warning, before any water appears; never wait to see water
Five steps to seal a room in a chemical release (Ready.gov)	Get inside; close and lock windows and doors; turn off fans and AC; go to an interior room; seal gaps with plastic sheeting and duct tape
What are plastic sheeting and duct tape for, and not for?	For sealing a room in a chemical event; useless against flood water or wind
Where should you be in an apartment during a tornado or hurricane wind?	An interior room with no windows, low floor, but never below ground if flooding is possible; cover head with mattress or cushions
Wind says go low, water says go high. Which wins when both are possible?	Water: never shelter below ground in a flood
The three-word rule after a nuclear explosion	Get inside, stay inside, stay tuned
How long may you have before fallout arrives, and how long should you stay inside?	10 minutes or more before fallout; stay inside at least 24 hours unless told otherwise
Best building and location for fallout shelter	Brick or concrete building, in the basement or the middle of a large building, away from outer walls, windows and roof
How many NYC hurricane evacuation zones are there and which is evacuated first?	Six zones; zone 1 is the highest risk and evacuated first; zone 6 lowest
Where to find your NYC evacuation zone	maps.nyc.gov/hurricane, or call 311 (video relay 212-639-9675, TTY 212-504-4115)
How do you sign up for Notify NYC by text?	Text NOTIFYNYC to 692-692; text your ZIP code to 692-692 for neighbourhood alerts
Four signs of hypothermia (Red Cross)	Shivering, confusion, slurred speech, drowsiness
Hypothermia treatment in order	Call 911; move to warmth; remove wet clothing; warm the core first (chest, neck, head, groin); warm drinks only if fully alert
Where do you warm a hypothermic person first?	The core: chest, neck, head and groin; not the arms and legs
Generator placement rule (CDC/CPSC)	Outdoors only, at least 20 feet from any window, door or vent, exhaust pointed away; never in a garage
What is carbon monoxide and how do you detect it indoors?	A colourless, odourless gas made by burning fuel; only a battery-powered CO alarm can detect it
Several people in the home get headaches and dizziness at the same time during a blackout. Action?	Get everyone outside to fresh air and call 911; do not go back in to look for the source
Can you heat an apartment with a gas oven, grill, camp stove or generator?	Never; all make carbon monoxide indoors and can kill you in your sleep
When is Code Blue in effect in NYC?	When the temperature including wind chill is at or below 32 F between 4 p.m. and 8 a.m.
What does Code Blue open to walk-ins?	Department of Homeless Services drop-in centers and shelters; more than 50 hospitals keep an open-door policy; call 311
How long does food stay safe in a fridge, full freezer and half-full freezer without power (doors shut)?	Fridge about 4 hours; full freezer about 48 hours; half-full freezer about 24 hours
When must perishable food be discarded after a power failure?	Above 40 F for more than 2 hours (1 hour if the room is above 90 F); when in doubt, throw it out
What happened to high-rise water in the 14 August 2003 blackout, and what should you do when power drops?	Pumps stopped and high-rises lost water pressure; fill the bathtub and every pot immediately
Signs of heat exhaustion (Red Cross)	Heavy sweating, weakness, nausea, cool clammy skin
Treatment for heat exhaustion	Move to a cool place, loosen clothing, sip water, rest, watch for confusion
Signs of heat stroke (Red Cross)	Confusion, hot skin (may stop sweating), temperature 103 F or above
Treatment for heat stroke	Call 911 and cool the person immediately: cool room, remove outer clothing, wet skin, cold cloths on neck, armpits and groin
Who must not be given fluids in a heat emergency?	Anyone confused or unconscious; they can choke. Cool the outside of the body instead
The one sign that turns heat illness into a 911 call	Confusion (or hot skin with a temperature of 103 F or above)
Does a fan cool the air in a hot apartment?	No; it only moves air. It helps with wet skin or cool night air; it is not a substitute for air conditioning
Night ventilation routine in a heat wave	Windows and blinds shut by day; after dark open windows on opposite sides with a fan blowing hot air out; close again at dawn
Where to find an NYC cooling center	finder.nyc.gov/coolingcenters or call 311; confirm on the day, centers change with each emergency
What does a proper heat-wave check on an elderly neighbour involve?	Knock twice a day, see them in person, ask if they are drinking water and if the room is cool; walk them somewhere cooler; 911 if confused
What is a go-bag?	One backpack per person, packed now, by the door, for leaving in minutes and managing about three days away from home
Ready.gov basic kit contents	Water, non-perishable food, hand-crank/NOAA radio, flashlight, first-aid kit, batteries, whistle, dust mask, plastic sheeting and duct tape, wipes and garbage bags, wrench, can opener, maps, chargers
Water storage rule and the goal	At least 1 gallon per person per day; a two-week supply (14 gallons) per person is the goal
Four things to add to the federal kit list for a New York go-bag	Copies of ID and documents in a zip bag, spare keys, cash in small bills, a week of medicines with a written list
Best-to-worst places to sleep in an evacuation	Family or friends outside the zone; a hotel booked early; a city evacuation center; a car outside the zone; a tent or tarp as last resort
Are NYC evacuation centers pet-friendly and where do you find the open ones?	Yes, pet-friendly and accessible; finder.nyc.gov/hurricane or 311; confirm at the time
Order of protection when sleeping outdoors	Something under you (bag, tarp, cardboard), something over you (blanket, dry layers, hat), something between you and the wind (tarp on cord)
Why is a tent in a park a last resort in NYC?	Parks are closed overnight and camping is not permitted; you are alone in the dark; centers, family or hotels come first
