What are the three steps of first aid, in order?	Check (the scene, then the person), Call 911, Care until help arrives
Adult CPR: rate, depth and ratio?	100-120 compressions a minute, 2-2.4 inches (5-6 cm) deep; 30 compressions to 2 breaths if giving breaths
What are the two steps of Hands-Only CPR?	Call 911, then push hard and fast in the centre of the chest at 100-120 a minute
How long do you check for breathing before starting CPR?	No more than 10 seconds; gasping is not breathing
A choking adult cannot speak, cough or breathe. What do you do?	5 back blows between the shoulder blades, then 5 abdominal thrusts inward and upward; repeat until it clears or they go limp
When should you NOT use the recovery position?	When you suspect a neck or back injury (fall from height, crash): keep the head still and wait for 911
Poison Control phone number and when to use it?	1-800-222-1222, free, 24 hours; for a swallowed poison or medicine when the person is awake and breathing (911 if not)
What does B.E. F.A.S.T. stand for?	Balance loss, Eye/vision changes, Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call 911
Cardiac arrest versus heart attack: what do you see?	Arrest: unresponsive, not breathing normally (CPR, AED). Heart attack: awake, chest pressure, arm/jaw pain, sweat (911, rest)
Since when has text-to-911 worked in NYC, and the rule?	June 2020: call if you can, text if you can't
Stop the Bleed: the three steps in order?	Direct pressure with both hands; pack a deep wound with gauze and press; tourniquet if a limb wound will not stop
Where does a tourniquet go?	2-3 inches above the wound, never on a joint; tighten until bleeding stops, write the time, do not remove
Why must you not lift the dressing to look at a bleeding wound?	It tears the forming clot; add more gauze on top and keep pressing
First aid for a burn: what to do and for how long?	Cool (not cold) running water for about 10 minutes, then cover loosely with a clean dry non-stick dressing
Name four things never to put on a burn.	Ice, butter, oil or petroleum jelly, toothpaste; and do not break blisters
Which burns need 911?	Full-thickness (dry, leathery, white/brown/black), larger than 3 inches, face/hands/feet/genitals/joints, electrical or chemical, smoke inhalation
What does RICE stand for in sprain care?	Rest, Ice (wrapped, 20 minutes at a time), Compression, Elevation above the heart
How do you handle a limb that looks bent after a fall?	Support it in the position found; do not straighten; splint only if you must move the person; nothing to eat or drink
How often does the CDC say adults need a tetanus booster?	Every 10 years; sooner check after a dirty puncture wound
Heat exhaustion versus heat stroke: the deciding signs?	Exhaustion: cool clammy skin, sweating, weak. Stroke: hot skin, confusion, 103 F or more; 911 and cool at once
Why not give water to a confused, overheated person?	They can choke; call 911 and cool the skin instead
CDC home oral rehydration solution recipe?	1 litre clean water, 6 level teaspoons sugar, half a level teaspoon salt; sip often, make fresh daily
Hypothermia: the signs and the warming rule?	Shivering, confusion, slurred speech, drowsiness; below 95 F. 911, remove wet clothes, warm chest, neck, head and groin first, gently
Frostbite: how do you rewarm it and what must you never do?	Warm (not hot) water comfortable to an unaffected hand; never rub, never snow, never a radiator, stove or heating pad
After a fire, which two signs mean 911 even if the person feels fine?	Hoarse voice and soot around the nose or mouth: the airway may swell shut hours later
The CDC's most common carbon monoxide symptoms?	Headache, dizziness, weakness, upset stomach, vomiting, chest pain, confusion; often called flu-like
How do you tell CO poisoning from flu?	No fever, everyone at home ill at once (pets too), better outdoors, worse at home, started after a fuel-burning appliance ran
First two actions when you suspect carbon monoxide?	Get everyone out into fresh air immediately, then call 911 from outside
Where must a generator run?	Outdoors only, at least 20 feet from any window, door or vent, exhaust pointed away; never in a garage, basement or hallway
NYC CO alarm law: who installs it and where?	Local Law 7 of 2004: the owner installs one in every apartment within 15 feet of each sleeping-room entrance; 311 if missing
FDA rule for insulin without a fridge?	Vials or cartridges keep 59-86 F for up to 28 days; never freeze; above 86 F it loses strength
Three ways to build a 30-day medication reserve?	Ask for a 90-day supply; refill on the first allowed day every month; ask for an emergency or vacation supply; rotate oldest first
Con Edison Life-Sustaining Equipment program: number and requirement?	1-877-582-6633 (or online/mail); medical certification by a doctor, NP, PA or health official; renewed yearly
Does Con Edison registration restore your power first?	No: it gives advance notice and outreach in outages. You still need backup cylinders or batteries and a place to go
What must someone on an oxygen concentrator have before a blackout?	Backup cylinders from the supplier that need no power, the hours they last written down, spare batteries, a plan to leave, 911 before it runs out
Pills got wet in flood water. What does the FDA say?	They are contaminated: discard and replace; only pills that stayed fully dry inside a wet container may be used until replaced
What is Rx Open?	Healthcare Ready's free map of open, closed and restricted pharmacies, dialysis centers and Red Cross shelters in a disaster area
What goes on a medical card?	Name, birth date, conditions, every medication and dose, allergies, blood thinners, doctor and pharmacy numbers, two contacts, insurance, devices
Handwashing rule and the sanitizer rule?	Soap and water at least 20 seconds; if no water, sanitizer with at least 60% alcohol (not on visibly dirty hands)
Baby under 3 months with a temperature of 100.4 F or higher?	Emergency: call the paediatrician immediately; if unreachable, go to an emergency department; do not medicate and wait
Four signs a wound is infected?	Spreading redness and warmth, pus, red streaks toward the heart, fever or chills (worse after day two)
Diarrhoea: which signs mean get care today?	Blood in stool or vomit, no urine over 8 hours, dizziness or confusion on standing, fever over 102 F, any baby under 12 months
Can a NYC public hospital emergency department refuse someone without insurance or papers?	No: EMTALA requires every ED to examine and stabilise an emergency; NYC Health + Hospitals treats regardless of pay or status and does not record status
What is NYC Health + Hospitals Virtual ExpressCare?	A 24/7 phone or video urgent-care visit for non-emergencies (cold, flu, minor infections, rashes); confirm the current number via 311
