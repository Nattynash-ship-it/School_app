How much water goes in the 72-hour kit for one adult?	1 gallon per person per day, 3 days minimum: 3 gallons (Ready.gov)
How much water does the two-week stay kit hold for one adult?	14 gallons: 1 gallon per day for 14 days (CDC two-week goal; Red Cross home target)
What are the first five items to buy or set up, in order?	Water, a way to hear alerts (NOAA radio, Notify NYC), a flashlight or headlamp, a first-aid kit, document copies
How does Ready.gov say to store the kit?	Items in airtight plastic bags, the whole kit in one or two easy-to-carry containers, in a place everyone knows
For how long does Ready.gov say to be ready to shelter at work?	At least 24 hours: food, water, medicines and comfortable walking shoes in a grab-and-go case
What does Ready.gov say to keep in a car kit?	Blankets, food and water, first aid and other items you may need if stranded; add a car charger and paper maps
What does NYC Emergency Management put in a Go Bag?	Bottled water, non-perishable food, document copies in a waterproof container, medications, flashlight, batteries, portable charger
What medication supply does the Red Cross recommend for the home kit?	A 1-month supply if possible
Where must a NYC apartment kit never be stored, and why?	The basement: 10 of the 14 people who died in NYC in Hurricane Ida (1-2 Sep 2021) drowned in basement apartments
When does Atlantic hurricane season run?	1 June to 30 November
What is the 1 June seasonal check?	Re-check your evacuation zone (nyc.gov/knowyourzone or 311), refresh the go-bag, confirm 14 days of water, top up cash
When is NYC heat season and what temperature must a landlord keep by day?	1 October to 31 May; 68 F by day when it is below 55 F outside, 62 F at night. No heat: 311
When does FDNY say to change removable batteries in smoke and CO alarms?	Twice a year, at the clock changes (March and November)
What kind of alarm does FDNY urge New Yorkers to install where people sleep?	A 10-year sealed-battery combination smoke and carbon monoxide alarm
How do you register life-support equipment with Con Edison?	Online at conEd.com/SpecialServices or call 1-877-582-6633; a medical professional must certify the equipment
How many days of supplies does NYC Emergency Management say to keep at home?	Up to seven days; this course builds to fourteen because high-rises can lose water pumps and elevators
Where is the free Ready New York: My Emergency Plan workbook?	nyc.gov/readyny, in 13 languages and audio
How often does Ready.gov say to hold a home fire drill, and what is the time target?	At least twice a year; practise getting out in under 2 minutes
How does FDNY say to make a home fire drill realistic?	Start in the sleeping area, darken the home as if smoke-filled, begin with the alarm sound, crawl low, go all the way to the meeting place
What must you never use during a fire drill or a real fire?	The elevator: it stops when power fails and fills with smoke. Take the stairs
What does the no-power evening usually reveal?	Dead batteries, a radio with no NOAA band, and how many hours the power bank really buys
What does the water-off day measure?	What one person really uses in a day against the 1-gallon-per-person planning figure, and whether stored water is reachable
Why practise the bleach dose on the water-off day?	8 drops (1/8 tsp) of unscented 5-9% bleach per gallon of clear water, 16 drops (1/4 tsp) if cloudy, wait 30 minutes: it must be automatic
When is the best month for the walk to your evacuation center?	May, before hurricane season starts on 1 June; use a center that has been used before and confirm via 311 or the finder at the time
Why does Ready.gov say to text rather than call in an emergency?	Texts need less bandwidth, get through when networks are jammed, and leave lines open for emergency workers
What is the family communication test?	A surprise text: 'Drill. Where would you go right now?' Everyone names the three meeting places; anyone who cannot gets a paper copy of the plan
What are the five debrief questions after a drill?	What was not there? What took longest? What could I not do? What if a child, pet, injured person or non-walking neighbour? Cheapest fix and when?
Which phone number must never be used for a test call?	911: it is for life-threatening emergencies only. Text-to-911 is 'call if you can, text if you can't'
What are the Red Cross's budget rules for building a kit?	Build it slowly, one item every time you shop; start with what you own; use sales, coupons and discount stores
What does month 1 of the twelve-month plan cost, and what does it include?	Only 3 gallons of water per person; the rest is free: Notify NYC, alerts on, out-of-town contact, paper plan, medical card, document photos, zone, alarm test
Which two dates can change the order of the twelve-month plan?	Hurricane season (1 June) pulls the go-bag month forward; heat season (1 October) pulls the warm-room month forward
Why is a generator the wrong purchase for an apartment?	It must run outdoors at least 20 feet from any window, door or vent (CDC); almost no apartment can do that, and the exhaust kills
How often must self-filled water containers be replaced?	Every 6 months (CDC); the plan pins it to 1 June and 1 December
How often does Ready.gov say to re-think the kit?	Re-think your needs every year and replace expired items as needed; the plan does it in September, National Preparedness Month
How often should the power bank be charged in maintenance?	To full once a month, on the 1st, with a phone reminder
When is the go-bag refreshed after the kit is built?	1 June (hurricane season) and 1 November (winter): food, water, clothes for the season, cash
What is the go-bag weight rule from the walk drill?	If it hurts by the halfway point it is too heavy; water is 8 pounds a gallon; aim for under 20 pounds
How is Notify NYC set up, and what does it cost?	Text NOTIFYNYC to 692-692, then text your ZIP code to 692-692 for neighbourhood alerts; free
