What three things should a child know first for an emergency?	How and when to dial 911; their address, phone number and parents' first and last names; the one out-of-town contact everyone texts
Why does Ready.gov say to text rather than call in a disaster?	Texts get through when calls fail and keep phone lines free for 911; talk only if in immediate danger
Who will an NYC public school release a child to during an early dismissal?	Only adults listed on the Emergency Contact (blue) Card, and they must show ID
Where can she update her child's school emergency contacts online?	NYC Schools Account at schoolsaccount.nyc, under Emergency Contacts
What are the four responses in the NYC schools General Response Protocol?	Evacuation, Shelter-In, Hold, Lockdown
Who should she call for information about a specific school during an emergency?	311 (Video Relay 212-639-9675, TTY 212-504-4115); also register for Notify NYC
How many days of medication does Ready.gov say an older or disabled person should keep on hand?	At least seven days of medicines and medical supplies, plus a written list of medicines, doses and allergies
What should one person in an elder's support network have and know?	A spare key, where the emergency supplies are, and how to use lifesaving equipment or give medicine
What is a Special Medical Needs Shelter?	A City shelter for evacuees who need help such as oxygen, wound care or vital-sign monitoring but not a hospital; has clinical staff
How does a person with a disability get transport out of an evacuation zone in NYC?	Call 311 once the Mayor orders an evacuation; accessible vehicle to an accessible center, or ambulance to a hospital outside the zone
What is the Con Edison phone number to register life-sustaining medical equipment?	1-877-582-6633; equipment must be certified by a medical professional; registrants get outage alerts and wellness checks
What is Con Edison Senior Direct?	A line for elderly, blind and disabled customers: 800-404-9097, Monday to Friday 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.
Elevator out, elder on the 9th floor cannot do stairs: what is the rule?	Bring supplies up, do not bring her down; check twice a day; confusion, hot skin or lost equipment power means 911
Signs of heat stroke in a neighbour, and the action?	Confusion, hot skin, temperature 103 F or above, may stop sweating: call 911, cool immediately, no fluids if confused
Ready.gov's rule on pets when officials order an evacuation?	Your pet evacuates too; pets left behind may end up lost, injured or worse
How much pet food and water does the ASPCA say to keep in a disaster kit?	Three to seven days per pet
Why keep a photo of yourself with your pet in the kit?	Ready.gov: it documents ownership and helps others identify the pet if you are separated
Are pets allowed at NYC evacuation centers?	Yes, pets and service animals are allowed at City shelters; bring food, leash, carrier and medication
What goes on an ASPCA pet rescue alert sticker?	The type and number of pets inside and your vet's phone number; put it on the door or a window
How do you prepare a cat that hides from the carrier?	Leave the carrier open by the door with a towel, feed the cat in it, close the door briefly, practise monthly
What is the 'two minutes then we go' rule?	Search for a hiding pet for a set short time, then leave with the child; never send anyone back inside
Signs of overheating in a pet (ASPCA)?	Excessive panting or difficulty breathing, fast heart and breathing, drooling, weakness, stupor, collapse, seizures; temperature over 104 F
Which pets overheat fastest?	Flat-faced breeds such as pugs and Persian cats; also elderly, overweight animals and those with heart or lung disease
Should a long-haired dog be shaved for a heat wave?	Trim, but never shave to the skin; the coat protects against overheating and sunburn
First aid for suspected frostbite on a pet?	Bring it inside, warm the area gently with warm never hot water, do not rub, see a vet promptly
Why wipe a dog's paws after a winter walk in NYC?	Ice-melt and rock salt can irritate, sicken or kill an animal that licks them off
How much does 24 hours awake impair judgement, per NIOSH?	About the same as a 0.10% blood alcohol level, above the legal driving limit; 17 hours is about 0.05%
What is the two-person rule?	Say any irreversible decision out loud to another person, or text the out-of-town contact, before doing it
Name the eight core actions of psychological first aid (NCTSN).	Contact and engagement; safety and comfort; stabilization; information gathering; practical assistance; connection with social supports; information on coping; linkage with services
What should you not do when giving psychological first aid?	Do not force the person to describe the event, tell them what to feel, or promise what you cannot deliver; never leave someone who talks of not wanting to live
NCTSN's five actions for children after a disaster?	Reassure them they are safe (repeat it); limit media exposure; keep routines; keep family rules; spend extra time and stay connected
How do you reach the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline?	Call or text 988, or chat at 988lifeline.org; free, confidential, 24/7; for any emotional distress, not only suicide
What is the SAMHSA Disaster Distress Helpline?	1-800-985-5990, call or text, 24/7, for distress after any disaster; no identifying information needed; Spanish press 2
How do you reach NYC 988 (formerly NYC Well)?	Call 988 or 1-888-NYC-WELL (1-888-692-9355), text WELL to 65173, or chat at nyc988.cityofnewyork.us; 24/7/365
What does NYC 988 offer beyond a phone call?	Crisis counselling, referrals, appointment help, mobile crisis teams, short-term counselling, peer support and follow-up, regardless of insurance or immigration status
When is it 911 rather than 988?	When someone is in immediate danger: has taken something, has a weapon, or is about to act
Cheapest sleep equipment for a shelter or blackout?	Foam earplugs and a fabric eye mask; sleep in four-hour shifts if there are two adults
