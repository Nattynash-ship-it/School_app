What marking shows safety glasses meet the ANSI eye-protection standard?	Z87 stamped on the frame or lens (ANSI Z87.1); ordinary glasses and sunglasses are not rated
What share of eye injuries does Prevent Blindness say the right safety eyewear could prevent?	About 90 percent
Which fastener resists being pulled straight out of wood better, screws or nails?	Screws: their threads grip the wood, so they resist withdrawal far better
Which fastener handles sideways (shear) force better, screws or nails?	Nails: they bend instead of snapping; screws can fail suddenly in shear
Where should screws go when covering an opening with a board?	Into solid wood (the window frame or a stud), never into plaster, drywall or trim alone
What do you do before changing a bit in a cordless drill?	Remove the battery
Which direction do you cut with a utility knife?	Away from your body, free hand behind the blade, blade retracted when you stop
Why must you not run a drill or strike a nail in a room that smells of gas?	Motors and metal on metal can spark and ignite the gas; leave and call 911 or Con Edison from outside
Which two utility tools does Ready.gov list in the basic emergency kit?	A wrench or pliers to turn off utilities, plus plastic sheeting and duct tape
What is the weight limit rule for the go-bag tool roll?	Lighter than a full water bottle: multi-tool, tape, cord, zip ties, screws, gloves, spare glasses, headlamp
Which way do you turn a water shut-off valve to close it?	Clockwise until it stops
What is a fixture stop?	The small shut-off valve under a sink or behind a toilet that stops water to that one fixture
Who controls the water main and gas meters in an apartment building?	The building (super or management); tenants control fixture stops and their own breaker panel
What do you do if a corroded under-sink valve will not turn?	Do not force it; bucket and towel under the leak, call the super to close the riser or main
Who do you call about water bubbling out of the street or a sudden pressure drop?	311 and ask for DEP (NYC Department of Environmental Protection); 311 also at 212-639-9675, TTY 212-504-4115
Why must you never put a wrench on the sidewalk curb valve?	It belongs to the city system; breaking it turns a city repair into a private one
Con Edison's rule when you smell gas?	Leave immediately with everyone, no phones, switches, matches or appliances, call 911 or 1-800-75-CONED (1-800-752-6633) from outside
Who turns gas back on after it has been shut off at a meter?	Only the utility or a qualified professional, after checking lines and appliances; never the customer
What is Con Edison's gas emergency phone number?	1-800-75-CONED, which is 1-800-752-6633
In what order does Ready.gov say to shut off electricity at the panel?	Individual circuit breakers first, then the main
When must you not touch a breaker panel at all?	When there is water on the floor or the panel is wet; leave, call the super, 911 if it sparks or smokes
What does NYC Emergency Management say about lights in a flooded basement?	Do not turn on lights or appliances until a qualified professional says it is safe
Does taping a window stop it breaking in a storm?	No. FEMA says tape does not prevent breakage; shutters are best, 5/8-inch plywood cut to fit is an option
What thickness of plywood does FEMA name for boarding windows?	5/8-inch, cut to fit and ready to install
What rule applies to any patch over a fire escape window?	It must not block the window; FDNY requires it kept clear, and no window guards may be fitted there
How full should a sandbag be filled?	Half to two-thirds full, about 35-40 pounds; a packed bag is 65-70 pounds and leaves gaps
How are sandbag rows laid?	Like bricks, each row offset by half a bag, flap folded and tucked under, plastic sheeting on the water side
How much moving water can knock over an adult, per NYC Emergency Management?	Six inches
What should a basement-apartment resident do when a flood warning arrives?	Move immediately to a place higher than the flood water and leave belongings behind
How many of the 14 New Yorkers killed by Hurricane Ida drowned in basement apartments?	10, on 1 September 2021
How does Operation Blue Roof hold a tarp on a damaged roof?	Wood strips nailed or screwed through the sheeting along every edge; grommets alone tear out
Why does one loose tarp edge matter in wind?	Wind gets underneath and the tarp becomes a sail, ripping itself and its anchors free
Quick way to brace a door that will not lock tonight?	Rubber wedge under it and a chair back or a 2x4 cut to fit jammed under the knob; long screws through the strike plate into framing
Who do you tell about a broken apartment door lock if the landlord does not fix it?	311, which routes it to HPD (Housing Preservation and Development); 911 first if someone may be inside
How do you join the Notify NYC basement flood warnings?	Text NOTIFYNYC to 692-692, then join the Basement Alert Group
What is the minimum breaking strength of Type III 550 paracord?	550 pounds for new cord in a straight pull, per military specification MIL-C-5040H
How many inner strands does 550 paracord have?	Seven nylon core strands inside a woven sheath; they pull out as thread or fishing line
What must paracord never be used for?	Lowering or holding a person; people use stairs, the fire escape or wait for FDNY
Which knot makes a fixed loop that will not slip or jam?	The bowline
Which knot joins two ropes of different thickness?	The sheet bend; both tails end on the same side, thicker rope forms the bight
Which knot lets you tighten or loosen a tarp line without untying it?	The taut-line hitch
What is the everyday hitch for tying a line to a post, ring or rail?	Two half hitches
Which knot starts a lashing that binds two poles?	The clove hitch, finished with a half hitch so it cannot roll
What is the square knot for, and what is it not for?	Tying bandages and closing bundles or parcels; not for joining ropes that carry load
How do you tie a line to a tarp corner that has no grommet?	Fold the corner over a small stone and tie a sheet bend around the neck
