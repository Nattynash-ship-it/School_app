What is the rule of threes?	A survival memory aid: about 3 minutes without air, 3 hours without shelter in harsh conditions, 3 days without water, 3 weeks without food. Not exact; a priority order.
In an apartment, what is the first thing the rule of threes tells you to spend money on?	Air: batteries for the smoke alarm and a battery-powered carbon monoxide detector.
What do the letters in S.T.O.P. stand for?	Stop, Think, Observe, Plan; then do one action and repeat.
Why is the first step of S.T.O.P. to stand still?	Because the first mistake in an emergency is usually moving by habit (elevator, going back for things) before thinking.
What does a green VERIFIED box in this course mean?	Every number in the lesson was checked against the named source on the date shown; open the link to see it yourself.
What does a grey NOT VERIFIED box mean and what should you do?	The fact could not be confirmed; confirm before you rely on it with the person it names (311, a doctor, the label).
911 or 311: who do you call for no heat in your apartment?	311 (after telling the super or owner). 911 is only for life-threatening emergencies.
How do you enrol in Notify NYC by text?	Text NOTIFYNYC to 692-692; text your ZIP code to 692-692 for neighbourhood alerts.
What is the 311 number from outside New York City?	212-639-9675 (not toll free). Text 311-692. TTY 212-504-4115.
Since when has Text-to-911 worked in NYC, and what is the rule?	Since June 2020. Call if you can, text if you can't.
Why do texts get through in a disaster when calls do not?	A text needs far less network capacity and can queue and send when capacity frees up; long-distance often works when local is jammed.
How long may you have to escape once a smoke alarm sounds, according to NFPA?	As little as one or two minutes.
How often does NFPA say to practise the home fire escape?	At least twice a year, once during the day and once at night.
How much fast-moving water can knock an adult down, and how much moves most cars?	6 inches knocks an adult down; 12 inches carries away most cars. Turn Around, Don't Drown.
How much water should be stored per person and what is the two-week goal?	At least 1 gallon per person per day; the goal is 14 gallons per person.
When is the Atlantic hurricane season and when is its peak?	1 June to 30 November; statistical peak on 10 September, most activity mid-August to mid-October.
How many NYC hurricane evacuation zones are there and which is evacuated first?	Six. Zone 1 is the highest risk and evacuated first; Zone 6 is the lowest.
Where do you find your NYC hurricane evacuation zone?	maps.nyc.gov/hurricane, nyc.gov/knowyourzone, or call 311.
How many people died in NYC from Hurricane Ida (Sept 2021) and where did most drown?	14 died; 10 of them drowned in basement apartments.
How hard did rain fall in Ida compared with what NYC sewers were designed for?	3.5 inches per hour, about double the 1.75 inches per hour design capacity.
What does NYC Emergency Management tell basement-apartment residents to do about flash floods?	Know your exits and move to a higher floor before the water arrives; join the Notify NYC basement alert group.
How many storm-surge deaths did Hurricane Sandy cause in New York State?	48 deaths in New York State (147 overall), mostly from storm surge, some from falling trees.
About how many New Yorkers die from heat each year?	About 500; the 2014-2023 annual average of heat-exacerbated deaths (May-September) was 489.
What triggers an NWS Heat Advisory for New York?	Heat index 95-99 F for at least two consecutive days, or 100-104 F for any period.
What triggers an NWS Extreme Heat Warning for New York?	Heat index of at least 105 F for at least two consecutive hours (renamed from Excessive Heat Warning in March 2025).
What are the signs of heat stroke and what do you do?	Confusion, hot skin, temperature 103 F or above, may stop sweating. Call 911, cool at once, no fluids if confused or unconscious.
Where do you find NYC cooling centres?	finder.nyc.gov/coolingcenters or call 311 (open during heat emergencies).
When did the 2003 blackout start and how many people did it affect?	14 August 2003, a few minutes after 4 p.m.; an area of about 50 million people. NYC power fully back 16 August.
How many people were stranded in NYC subway cars in the 2003 blackout?	Up to 400,000; all 11,600 traffic signals also failed.
Stuck in an elevator during a blackout: what do you do?	Press the alarm or help button, stay inside, make noise every few minutes; never pry doors or climb out because the car can restart.
How do you report a power outage to Con Edison?	Call 1-800-752-6633 (1-800-75-CONED), report at coned.com, or text REG to 688-243 for updates.
How far from windows and doors must a generator be?	Outdoors only, at least 20 feet from any window, door or vent, exhaust pointed away; never in a garage or on a fire escape.
How long does food stay safe in a shut fridge and freezer without power?	Fridge about 4 hours; full freezer about 48 hours; half-full freezer about 24 hours. Discard perishables above 40 F for more than 2 hours.
When is NYC Heat Season and what temperature must landlords keep?	1 October to 31 May; at least 68 F inside when it is below 55 F outside; hot water at least 120 F all year.
What is Code Blue in NYC?	In effect when the temperature including wind chill is at or below 32 F between 4 p.m. and 8 a.m.; shelters accept walk-ins; call 311 for someone outside.
If you smell gas, whom do you call and from where?	From outside: 911, then the gas company: Con Edison 1-800-752-6633 or National Grid 718-643-4050 (Brooklyn, Queens, Staten Island).
What are Ready.gov's shelter-in-place steps for a chemical release?	Go inside, close and lock windows and doors, turn off fans and A/C, go to an interior room, seal gaps with plastic sheeting and duct tape.
What is the rule for a nuclear event?	Get inside, stay inside, stay tuned; brick or concrete building, basement or middle of a large building; stay in at least 24 hours unless told otherwise.
Who should your out-of-area contact be?	One reliable person outside the New York area whom every household member texts with an agreed short message: name, OK or HURT, where, where going.
What are the three meeting places in the household plan?	A fixed spot across the street from the building; a place outside the neighbourhood (library, place of worship); a place outside the city reachable on foot or by bus.
What is the difference between a stay kit and a go bag?	Stay kit: several days of water, food, light, radio, first aid at home. Go bag: one sturdy backpack per person with water, ID, medications, chargers, documents.
Where should the written household plan be kept?	Three paper copies: fridge or cabinet door, go bag, wallet; plus a photo sent to the out-of-area contact.
Where do you get the Ready New York: My Emergency Plan workbook?	nyc.gov/readyny (13 languages and audio) or call 311 (TTY 212-504-4115) for a paper copy.
Why count the doors between your apartment and the stairwell?	In smoke you cannot see exit signs; you find the stairs by counting doors by touch.
