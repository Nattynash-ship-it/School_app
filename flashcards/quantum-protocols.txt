What resources does superdense coding consume and what does it deliver?	1 ebit (a shared Bell pair) + 1 transmitted qubit → 2 classical bits.
What resources does teleportation consume and what does it deliver?	1 ebit + 2 classical bits → 1 qubit state moved from Alice to Bob.
Write the four Bell states.	Φ⁺ = (|00⟩+|11⟩)/√2, Φ⁻ = (|00⟩−|11⟩)/√2, Ψ⁺ = (|01⟩+|10⟩)/√2, Ψ⁻ = (|01⟩−|10⟩)/√2.
In superdense coding, which gate does Alice apply for message b₁b₂?	Z^b₁ X^b₂ on her qubit: 00→I, 01→X, 10→Z, 11→X then Z.
What circuit performs a Bell measurement?	CNOT (first qubit control), then H on the first qubit, then measure both in the computational basis.
Superdense coding, message 01: what does Qiskit print?	'10', because Qiskit bitstrings are little-endian (qubit 0 is the rightmost character).
Why does superdense coding not violate the Holevo bound?	Bob measures two qubits (d = 4, at most 2 bits); one of them was the pre-shared half of the ebit.
Holevo bound for a d-dimensional system sent alone?	At most log₂ d classical bits accessible: 1 bit per qubit.
What is Bob's reduced state for any Bell state?	I/2, the maximally mixed state: local measurements give 50/50 results.
In superdense coding, what goes wrong if Bob forgets the final H?	The first bit becomes random (50/50); for message 01 he sees 01 or 11 with probability 0.5 each.
Teleportation: state after CNOT and H, grouped by Alice's bits?	(1/2) ∑ |m₁m₂⟩ ⊗ X^m₂ Z^m₁ |ψ⟩.
Teleportation: Bob's correction for outcome m₁m₂?	Z^m₁ X^m₂: apply X if m₂ = 1, then Z if m₁ = 1.
Teleportation: probability of each of Alice's four outcomes?	1/4 each, independent of α and β, so Alice learns nothing about |ψ⟩.
Why is teleportation not faster than light?	Until the 2 classical bits arrive, Bob's averaged state is I/2, identical to before Alice acted.
Why does teleportation not violate no-cloning?	Alice's measurement leaves her qubits in |m₁m₂⟩; the original state is destroyed, so only Bob holds |ψ⟩.
Teleporting 0.6|0⟩ + 0.8i|1⟩, outcome 10: Bob's uncorrected state?	Z|ψ⟩ = 0.6|0⟩ − 0.8i|1⟩; Bob applies Z.
Does the order of X and Z corrections matter in teleportation?	Only up to a global phase: XZ = −ZX, and a global −1 is unobservable.
What is the deferred measurement principle?	Measure-then-classically-control equals quantum-control-then-measure; used to simulate teleportation with CX and CZ.
What is entanglement swapping?	A Bell measurement on B1, B2 from pairs A–B1 and B2–C leaves A and C entangled though they never interacted.
Why does entanglement swapping matter for networks?	Chaining swaps across stations extends entanglement over long distances: the core of a quantum repeater.
State the CHSH combination S.	S = E(0,0) + E(0,1) + E(1,0) − E(1,1), with E the average product of ±1 outcomes.
Classical and quantum bounds on |S|?	Local hidden variables: 2. Quantum (Tsirelson): 2√2 ≈ 2.8284. Algebraic maximum: 4.
CHSH game winning condition?	a ⊕ b = x · y: answers agree unless both inputs are 1, then they must differ.
Winning probability of the CHSH game: classical vs quantum?	Classical 0.75; quantum cos²(π/8) ≈ 0.8536. In general P_win = 1/2 + S/8.
Correlator E(a,b) for Φ⁺ with Bloch angles a, b in the x-z plane?	E = cos(a − b). For the singlet Ψ⁻ it is −cos(a − b).
Optimal CHSH Bloch angles for Φ⁺?	Alice 0° (Z) and 90° (X); Bob 45° and −45°, i.e. (Z ± X)/√2.
Key step in proving |S| ≤ 2 for local hidden variables?	A₀(B₀+B₁) + A₁(B₀−B₁): one bracket is 0, the other ±2, for every λ.
Visibility needed to violate CHSH with v·Φ⁺ + (1−v)·I/4?	v > 1/√2 ≈ 0.7071, since S = 2√2·v.
Name three loopholes in Bell tests.	Locality (settings not space-like separated), detection/fair sampling (losses), freedom of choice (settings correlated with λ).
Delft 2015 loophole-free Bell test: system and result?	NV electron spins 1.3 km apart; 245 trials, S = 2.42 ± 0.20, P ≤ 0.039 (Hensen et al., Nature 2015).
Which other groups reported loophole-free Bell tests in 2015?	Vienna (Giustina et al., PRL 115, 250401) and NIST (Shalm et al., PRL 115, 250402), both with entangled photons.
Who won the 2022 Nobel Prize in Physics, and for what?	Aspect, Clauser and Zeilinger, for experiments with entangled photons establishing the violation of Bell inequalities.
Does a Bell violation allow signalling?	No: each party's marginal outcomes are 50/50 regardless of the other's setting.
Polariser angle versus Bloch angle for photons?	Bloch angle = 2 × polariser angle; optimal bench settings are 0°, 45° (Alice) and ±22.5° (Bob).
Standard deviation of an estimated S with N trials per setting pair?	≈ √(4(1 − E²)/N); about 0.18 for N ≈ 61 and E = 0.7071.
