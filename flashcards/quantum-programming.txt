In Qiskit, which character of a bitstring belongs to qubit 0?	The rightmost. Qiskit is little-endian: '01' means qubit 0 = 1, qubit 1 = 0.
Qiskit state-vector index of basis state with bits b0, b1, ..., b(n-1)?	index = sum of b_i · 2^i (qubit 0 is the least significant bit).
Which Qiskit class gives exact amplitudes of an unmeasured circuit?	qiskit.quantum_info.Statevector (e.g. Statevector.from_instruction(qc) or Statevector(qc)).
Which core-Qiskit primitive samples measured circuits shot by shot?	qiskit.primitives.StatevectorSampler; run([qc], shots=N).result()[0].data.<register>.get_counts().
What does the Estimator primitive return?	Expectation values ⟨ψ|O|ψ⟩ of observables such as SparsePauliOp('ZZ') for circuits without measurements.
What register name does measure_all() create?	'meas', so the counts are result[0].data.meas.get_counts().
What is a pub in Qiskit primitives?	A Primitive Unified Bloc: one circuit plus its observables/parameter values/shots, submitted in a list to run().
What happened to qiskit.execute()?	Deprecated in 0.46 and removed in Qiskit 1.0; use transpile plus primitives (or backend.run) instead.
Why can't Statevector.from_instruction take a circuit with measure_all()?	Measurement is not unitary; Qiskit raises 'Cannot apply instruction with classical bits: measure'.
Standard error of an outcome frequency with probability p and N shots?	√(p(1−p)/N); for p = 0.5 it is 0.0158 at N = 1,000 and 0.0079 at N = 4,000.
Bell state expectation values ⟨ZZ⟩, ⟨ZI⟩, ⟨XX⟩?	1, 0 and 1 (StatevectorEstimator output [1. 0. 1.]).
What four jobs does the transpiler do?	Layout, routing (insert SWAPs), translation to basis gates, and optimisation.
What is an ISA circuit?	A circuit already written in a backend's native gates and obeying its coupling map, ready for hardware primitives.
How many CX gates make one SWAP?	Three: CX(a→b) · CX(b→a) · CX(a→b).
Hadamard in the {rz, sx, x, cx} basis?	RZ(π/2) · SX · RZ(π/2) with global phase π/4 (verified with Operator.equiv).
What is a coupling map?	A graph of physical qubits whose edges are the pairs that support a two-qubit gate.
Estimated success probability (ESP) of a circuit?	Product over gates and measurements of (1 − error rate). Crude, but good for ranking circuits.
Define T1 and T2.	T1: relaxation time of |1⟩ to |0⟩. T2: dephasing time of superpositions. Physically T2 ≤ 2T1.
Survival probability of |1⟩ after time t with relaxation time T1?	e^(−t/T1); e.g. 0.8187 for t = 20 μs, T1 = 100 μs.
Why are RZ gates nearly free on superconducting chips?	They are done virtually by shifting the phase of later pulses, with no physical pulse of their own.
Why fix seed_transpiler?	Layout and routing are randomised heuristics; a seed makes the transpiled circuit reproducible.
Kraus operators of the single-qubit depolarizing channel with parameter p?	√(1−3p/4)·I, √(p/4)·X, √(p/4)·Y, √(p/4)·Z.
What is GenericBackendV2 for?	A synthetic backend in core Qiskit with random but realistic calibration data, for testing transpilation. Not a real device.
Where do you read a backend's gate errors and T1/T2 in Qiskit?	backend.target: target['cx'][(0, 1)].error, .duration, and target.qubit_properties[q].t1 / .t2.
Why did a GHZ 'star' circuit need 10 CX on a line chip but the 'chain' only 4?	The star's CXs from qubit 0 to distant qubits force SWAPs; the chain matches the line's edges.
IBM Open Plan free QPU time (checked 28 Sep 2026)?	Up to 10 minutes per 28-day rolling window, per IBM's plans overview.
Bit order in Cirq and PennyLane state vectors?	Big-endian: the first qubit/wire is the most significant bit, the opposite of Qiskit.
How do you reverse Qiskit's qubit order for comparison?	Statevector.reverse_qargs() or QuantumCircuit.reverse_bits().
What does OpenQASM 3 add over OpenQASM 2?	Classical types, control flow on mid-circuit results, subroutines, and timing instructions.
OpenQASM 3 syntax for measuring qubit 0 into bit 0?	c[0] = measure q[0];  (OpenQASM 2 wrote measure q[0] -> c[0];)
Qiskit function that writes OpenQASM 3 text?	qiskit.qasm3.dumps(circuit). Reading it back needs the optional qiskit_qasm3_import package.
What is Cirq's basic time-slice unit?	The Moment: a set of operations applied at the same time; a Circuit is a sequence of Moments.
What is PennyLane designed for?	Differentiable quantum programming: QNodes whose gradients plug into PyTorch, JAX or TensorFlow.
Parameter-shift rule for a Pauli-rotation angle θ?	∂⟨O⟩/∂θ = [⟨O⟩(θ + π/2) − ⟨O⟩(θ − π/2)]/2; two circuit runs per parameter.
What is in Microsoft's Quantum Development Kit?	The Q# language and compiler (mostly Rust), a VS Code extension, Python package, and a resource estimator.
Which clouds accept OpenQASM 3 or list multi-vendor QPUs (checked 28 Sep 2026)?	Amazon Braket accepts OpenQASM 3.0 programs; Azure Quantum lists IonQ, Pasqal, Quantinuum and Rigetti.
Reproducibility checklist for a hardware run?	Pinned versions, seeds, shots, transpiled circuit (as QASM), backend name, calibration time, bit-order convention.
